Welcome to Dyradia.

This is the whole thing (at least as much as I've recorded so far, definitely more to come) - the world, its history, its dead gods and its living politics, the people who run it and the people who are trying to survive it. It started as a forum roleplay in 2004 and even when that group fizzled out, I kept adding to it. I was hooked on what was created, and that has turned into a vast, sprawling world. Everything here is the working reference for the campaign, published so you can actually read it instead of waiting for me to get back to you on Discord.

Poke around. Follow links. Ask questions. This is my baby, and I'd love to share the world I've created and continue to add onto.

---

## A word about spoilers

**If you are currently playing in the campaign: this site contains things your character does not know.**

Not just a little. A **lot**. Antagonists, their plans, what's underneath the things you've been poking at, and in several cases the answer to a question your party is actively asking at the table.

I'm not going to lock it. You're adults, I trust you, and honestly the world is more fun when you can look things up. But understand the trade-off: I can't un-spoil something for you, and if you read ahead and the reveal lands flat six months from now, that's a thing you did to yourself and you'll have to sit with it.

Some of you will want to read everything. Some of you will want to stay in the dark. Both are correct. Just pick on purpose, and know what you're getting into.

**To make that easier, everything DM-facing is labelled.** Any page that could genuinely cost you something carries a red banner at the top, and the worst of it is gathered under [[Lore/DM-Only Lore/index|DM-Only Lore]] - a section you have to walk into deliberately. If you see the banner and you're playing, that's your cue to close the tab.

*(If you're here for a one-off, or you're not in the campaign at all - ignore all of this and read whatever you like. The DM-Only section is the good stuff.)*

## Where to start

**If you want a story, not a wiki** — start with [[Lore/Historical Lore/The Origin Chronicle/index|The Origin Chronicle]]. Twelve chapters, narrative prose, the events that broke the world. It's the most readable thing here and it doesn't require you to know anything first.

**If you're about to play** — read [[Lore/Current Lore/THE CORE WORLD STATES OF DYRADIA|The Core World States]] first, then whichever nation you're from: [[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Mehrendt|Mehrendt]], [[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Gohlstout|Gohlstout]], [[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Fey'thra|Fey'thra]], or [[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Avalon|Avalon]]. That's genuinely enough to show up and play.

**If you want to know how the world works** — [[Lore/Current Lore/State of Magic in Dyradia|the state of magic]], [[Lore/Historical Lore/Notable Places & Events/The Interstice|the Interstice]] and the Node network that made travel possible, and [[Lore/Current Lore/The Dyradian Calendar|the calendar]], because Dyradia does not count years the way you do.

**If you want the deep history** — [[Lore/Historical Lore/Notable Places & Events/Major Historical Eras|the eras]], then [[Lore/Historical Lore/Notable Places & Events/The Academic Awakening|the Academic Awakening]], then [[Lore/Historical Lore/Notable Places & Events/The Fracture|the Fracture]]. That's the spine: a golden age of connection, and the thing that ended it.

**If you want to know who's dangerous** — [[Lore/Historical Lore/Factions/Church of Prim|the Church of Prim]], and then the [[Lore/Current Lore/THE POLITICAL WEATHER OF DYRADIA|political weather]], which is my honest read on where the pressure is building right now.

## Some housekeeping

Everything here is a living document. Things get revised when the table discovers something, when I finally decide a thing I'd been avoiding, or when I realize past-me wrote something that no longer holds. If a page contradicts another page, the more specific one usually wins - and tell me, because I'd like to fix it.

Yeah, some links don't point to anything but a reference right now. Those are either to be determined, I haven't gotten to writing something proper for, or somewhere in-between.

Use the search. There's a lot here, and search is faster than clicking.
