# The Origin Chronicle

*The narrative reconstruction of the founding events of the Dyradian historical record — drawn from primary fragments, witness accounts, and the long memory of those who walked beside the principal actors.*

---

## About This Chronicle

The chapters below recount events from the years surrounding [[The Fracture]], chiefly centered on the lives of [[Ezericht La'Monte]], [[Edward Z Zwarf]], and the companions who shaped the late pre-Fracture and early-Fracture world. Where possible, the chronicler has preserved the cadence of original voice — recovered phrases, remembered turns of dialogue, the small details that survived the collapse of so much else.

The chronicle is in-world canonical history. It is not session material; it is the **bedrock the campaign sits upon**.

**Part One** is anchored at year **0 AF** — the Fracture year by the canonical reckoning. The events of these twelve chapters cover the immediate run-up to the regicide of King [[Alaric La'Monte]], the regicide itself, and the closing day on which the [[The Cardinal|Cardinal/Gheronda]] of the [[Church of Prim]] transformed it into the founding propaganda of the war that defines the era. Subsequent chronicles (Part Two and beyond) cover the war and its closing climax (~30-41 AF), the [[Maeleus]]/[[Ometrieysa]] detonation that ends the Church's high doctrine, and the Severing that closes the Fracture proper.

See [[Major Historical Eras]] for the canonical Anchor Events table aligned with this chronicle.

## Chapters

- [[Chapter 1 - The Anniversary Raid]] — Sahlen falls under attack on the night Ezericht marks the death of his adopted family. He meets Edward Z Zwarf and the necromancer known only as Six at the city's South Gate.
- [[Chapter 2 - The Monk and the Mission]] — The arrival of Zant the monk, the second wave of attack, the interrogation that reveals a city guard let the soldiers in, and the appearance of Trinity Stolen with the offer to rescue the King.
- [[Chapter 3 - The Contract and the Camp]] — Cain joins; the company signs the Veneficus contracts; Cain rides to the giant camp and discovers a massacre that exposes the raid as a diversion; Six fights a strange creature in an alley; Trinity flees to Valham.
- [[Chapter 4 - Of Stones and Stillvein]] — Zant steals the purple stone he came to Sahlen for; Cain returns at dawn with news of the massacre; ambush at Ezericht's shack; Six performs his blood-rite; Trinity dreams of death and returns to Sahlen to recruit Zant.
- [[Chapter 5 - Of Cat-Eyes and Contracts]] — The company convenes at the shack; Trinity insults Edward; Edward shoots her in the leg as warning; Trinity's non-human aspect surfaces (blue-white eyes, cat-pupils, brow-horns) as she heals; the three contracts are read aloud; Six and Cain sign; Edward draws Ezericht aside for a private word.
- [[Chapter 6 - The Amulet and the Dagger]] — Edward's true identity as Commander Zywarf revealed; the briefing with General Flitney; Ezericht learns he is the unofficial son of King Alaric and is sent to assassinate his own father to nullify the Church's contract.
- [[Chapter 7 - Of Stones and Brothers]] — The dispersal; Trinity's chain divides into four; Zant teleports east and loses his stone to a winged imp; Six returns to a swamp castle and his father dispatches the four elemental brothers (Natas, Marxus, Nazu, Zin) to assist.
- [[Chapter 8 - The Fourth Chamber]] — The ride west; the rune-sealed door whose marks match Ezericht's dagger; the King who knew his son's name; the strike; the spectre that rose from the throne; the rippling country southeast.
- [[Chapter 9 - The Hour the World Turned]] — Parallel threads: Trinity hears of the Dark Harbingers at Dunmisk; Natas burns a hotel and approaches Trinity; Marxus walks past the monastery's bowmen; Six feels the contract-completion pulse and dispatches Zin; the wave of earth that brings the cart down; Edward's fun-ride escape; Ezericht's separate escape east.
- [[Chapter 10 - Of Gems and Fire]] — The mission contract bursts into flame; Trinity dispatches Shift with nullification; Natas burns the southern town to ash; Marxus drowns Medinas at the monastery; Zin breaches the Western Barracks wall; Trinity binds the spectre in a red-turning white gem and speaks privately to the dead King.
- [[Chapter 11 - The Vanished Friend]] — Ezericht grabs Six's soul-stone and is paralyzed by its defense; Six stops Zin; Edward explains the dagger's true origin and rips up Six's contract; the war-map of the monastery's outposts; Ezericht reveals the relic of a childhood friend who was one of Six's father's earlier experiments and chose mortality back.
- [[Chapter 12 - The Coronation of Lies]] — **Part One Finale.** Trinity carries the body of King Alaric to Nohlle via Shift's fog-form; the Gheronda's public address from the balcony transforms the regicide into the founding propaganda of a religious-political war; the rest of the royal line is publicly declared dead at the lands of Marencia; the war is declared. *"Prepare, Trinity, for the war has started."*
