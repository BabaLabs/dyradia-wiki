# The Dark Harbingers

A cult of unknown size and unspecified doctrine, observed at a distance by the [[Hypostasis Monastery]] for some years before the period of the Origin Chronicle.

## Introduction in the Chronicle

The Dark Harbingers entered the chronicle by rumour, in the tavern of [[Dunmisk]], where two half-drunk patrons whispered that the cult had moved to the west and that *"their leader knows about the King's whereabouts."* The rumour was overheard by [[Trinity Stolen]]. See [[Chapter 9 - The Hour the World Turned]].

The monastery's prior assessment had been that the cult's operations were sufficiently small not to require direct attention. The Dunmisk rumour was the chronicle's first indication that that assessment had become out of date.
