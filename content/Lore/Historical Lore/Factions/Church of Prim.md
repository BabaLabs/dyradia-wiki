# Church of Prim

The dominant religious-political institution of late pre-Fracture and early-Fracture [[Mehrendt]]. Rose to power during the [[Major Historical Eras|Ascendancy of the Church]], engineered the political crisis that precipitated [[The Fracture]], and collapsed in the late Fracture-era when its high doctrine was annihilated in the [[Maeleus]]/[[Ometrieysa]] detonation.

## Quick Reference

- **Era of dominance:** Late Bohrovan → Early Fracture (~210 BF – ~41 AF)
- **Stronghold:** [[Mehrendt]] (with extensions across Dyradia)
- **Public leadership:** [[The Cardinal]] (Absent Authority), [[Ometrieysa]] (Grand Priestess), [[Thye'Roscia]] (Arch-Bishop), [[I'ada]] (Elder Matriarch), [[Menogene]] (Apostle)
- **Inner order:** [[Hypostasis Monastery]] of the western borders (covert political-magical operations)
- **Status at Campaign Present (327 AF):** institutionally fractured, but residual influence persists through doctrine, customs, and surviving clergy. Remnant authority centered at [[The Pale Seat]] (Sanctum Primaris).

## Structure: Public Face and Inner Order

The Church of Prim operated as **two structurally distinct but coordinated bodies** under a single supreme authority.

### Public Church

The public-facing Church based in [[Mehrendt]] — Cardinal, Grand Priestess, Arch-Bishop, Elder Matriarch, Apostle, the visible clerical hierarchy, the temples, the doctrine, the public ceremony. Its operations were the religious life of late pre-Fracture Mehrendt: confession, sacrament, instruction, the political pressure on civic authority, the manufacture of public sentiment, and the formal Church military arm whose involvement in the Holy Crusades of approximately twenty years before the Fracture was canonized only in private record.

### The Hypostasis Inner Order

The **[[Hypostasis Monastery]]** of the western borders functioned as the Church's **covert political-magical arm** — the inner order through which the Church's true political objectives were planned and executed. The Hypostasis administered the [[Veneficus Contracts]] tradition, ran the covert intelligence operations against the Mehrendt royal court, and was the operational seat of the Supreme Voice's parallel hierarchy. Field agents of the Hypostasis (e.g. [[Trinity Stolen]]) reported to the order's senior — addressed by the title **Gheronda** — and operated independently of the public Church's chain of command.

### The Supreme Voice

The **same man** held the title [[The Cardinal|Cardinal]] in the public Church and **Gheronda** in the Hypostasis. His two operational identities allowed him to remain *Absent Authority* publicly while running the inner order privately, and — critically — to survive the late-Fracture detonation that annihilated the rest of the Church's high doctrine: he was not at the central ritual because his real seat was at the Hypostasis HQ in the west.

## The Veneficus Contract Tradition

The Church's distinctive magical-administrative instrument was the **[[Veneficus Contracts]]** tradition — magical binding contracts (in blood, soul, or combined form) whose violation drew the violator's existence into a limbo dimension. The tradition was administered by the Hypostasis arm and used both for internal Church discipline and for the Church's external political agreements — including the **ten-year reform contract** the Cardinal/Gheronda bound King [[Alaric La'Monte]] to in the period preceding the regicide.

The Church's contract on King Alaric followed the same self-burning property as the Veneficus instruments: at the moment of Alaric's death, the contract and all copies of it were destroyed by their own magic, leaving no evidentiary record. The Hypostasis tradition and the Church's broader contract magic share a deeper origin not yet specified by the chronicle.

## The Plan Against the Crown

The Church's principal political project in the years preceding the Fracture was the systematic acquisition of legislative control over [[Mehrendt]] through the King. The mechanism was a ten-year reform contract bound on King [[Alaric La'Monte|Alaric]] — under circumstances the chronicle's later record describes as unflattering — that would have transferred full legislative authority into the Church's hands by the contract's maturation date.

The Mehrendt military, having uncovered the contract through intelligence operations against the Hypostasis, took King Alaric into protective custody. The Church's response involved two parallel actions:

1. **The propagandized civil war** — a manufactured conflict on Mehrendt's southern border intended to draw the King out of hiding by leveraging his concern for his subjects.
2. **The bloodline-bind trap** — once the war's pressure failed, the Church placed a magical bloodline-bind on the King's holding chamber that could only be released by another royal bloodline. The bind was presented to the military's intelligence as a *defensive enchantment* the Church had placed for the King's own protection. It was in fact a *trigger* — designed to surface the unknown bastard heir whose location had eluded the Church for years. The military's last-resort plan — to use the bastard heir to kill the King and dissolve the contract — was the action the Church had been waiting for. The regicide identified the heir. See [[Chapter 8 - The Fourth Chamber]] for the act and [[Chapter 12 - The Coronation of Lies]] for the Gheronda's confirmation that the heir was now known.

The Church's planning was thorough, multi-decade, and patient. The public propaganda war the Cardinal/Gheronda declared at Nohlle the evening of the regicide was the *third* stage of the operation, not the first.

## The Church's War Against Magic

The Church of Prim's public-facing program of anti-magic conditioning predated the events of the Origin Chronicle by roughly two decades. The **Holy Crusades of approximately twenty years before the Fracture** were a Church-orchestrated military campaign — fought publicly under regular-army flags but staffed substantially by Hypostasis monks in soldier-cover — that primed public opinion against uncontrolled magic users.

This conditioning paid out fully when [[Edward Z Zwarf]] — the Awoken fire-adept whose Awoken signature had been masked for years by the Mehrendt military's turquoise amulets — *broke* in the post-Fracture chaos and burned a district of [[Nohlle]] off the map in uncontrolled rage. The Church did not need to manufacture the resulting anti-magic war. They had been preparing the kindling for twenty years. Z provided the spark, and the Church responded with the war they had been ready to declare for half a generation.

See [[The Awoken]] for the broader context of the magical phenomenon the Church's war was officially directed against.

## The Detonation and the Fall

The Church's high doctrine — the public clerical hierarchy minus the Cardinal — was annihilated in the late-Fracture climax (~30-41 AF) by the controlled magical detonation of [[Ometrieysa]] (whom the Church had broken and rebuilt as a holy weapon) anchored by [[Maeleus]] (the deep-cover Mehrendt general whose entire adult life had been spent infiltrating the Church to reach her). The two of them — both casualties of the Church's machinery, both reaching across the destruction the Church had made of them — chose to end their own lives in a way that took the Church's doctrinal head with them, in the process preventing the cataclysmic ritual the Church had been preparing.

The Cardinal — at the Hypostasis HQ rather than at the central ritual — survived. The Church's public face collapsed. The Hypostasis went quiet. The remnant Church authority concentrated at [[The Pale Seat]] under uncertain leadership.

The institution that remains in the campaign present is the long shadow of what the Church was. The Cardinal is presumed dead by most surviving authorities. The chronicle records that *presumed* is a careful word.
