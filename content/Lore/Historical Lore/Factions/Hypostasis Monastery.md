# Hypostasis Monastery

The **covert inner order of the [[Church of Prim]]**, based at the western borders of [[Mehrendt]] and active in the years surrounding [[The Fracture]]. By their own admission to those they recruited, *"long been involved in the ruling of Dyradia, secretly nevertheless."*

## Position Within the Church of Prim

The Hypostasis is the **operational covert arm** of the public Church of Prim — administered by the same supreme authority (the Cardinal in public, the Gheronda in inner-order title) but operating independently of the public Church's clerical hierarchy. The public Church handles doctrine, ceremony, civic pressure, and the visible religious life of Mehrendt; the Hypostasis handles the contracts, the intelligence operations, the political maneuvering, and the magical instruments by which the Church's actual political objectives are pursued.

The monastery's outward face is religious. Its inward face is political. To outsiders the Hypostasis presents as a contemplative order of monks observing standard ascetic disciplines; to those it touches directly, the Hypostasis is an agent of governance, operating through influence and intermediation rather than direct rule.

See [[Church of Prim]] for the full structural relationship between public Church and Hypostasis inner order.

## Internal Structure

The chronicle preserves one detail of the monastery's internal hierarchy: the title **Gheronda**, used as the respectful form of address for an elder. [[Trinity Stolen]] reported to her Gheronda through a creature-companion called [[Shift]], suggesting that Hypostasis field-agents maintained near-immediate communication with the monastery's senior figures even at significant distance.

## Known Operations

- **The Recruitment at [[Sahlen]].** Trinity Stolen was dispatched into Mehrendt during the Sahlen raids to identify and recruit a company capable of attempting the **rescue of the King**. The candidates she identified — [[Ezericht La'Monte]], [[Edward Z Zwarf]], [[Six]], and an unnamed companion — were offered fifteen hundred gold each, plus a five-hundred-gold supplement for additional hands. See [[Chapter 2 - The Monk and the Mission]].

## The Capital Outpost at Nohlle

The monastery maintained, in addition to its western-borders headquarters, a **capital outpost** at the central plaza of [[Nohlle]] — the seat of the public-facing operations under direct supervision of the Gheronda. The Nohlle outpost featured an audience chamber whose high narrow window overlooked the city's harbour, and a balcony from which the order's senior delivered public addresses to the assembled citizens. The Gheronda's foundational propaganda address transforming the regicide of King [[Alaric La'Monte|Alaric]] into the founding pretext of a religious-political war was delivered from the Nohlle balcony at the close of [[Chapter 12 - The Coronation of Lies]].

## The Gheronda — and the Cardinal

The senior of the order — addressed by the title *Gheronda* and by his junior agents (where they presumed) as *old man* — is the architect of the contract by which the Church had bound King [[Alaric La'Monte]] to a ten-year programme of legislative reform. His name has not yet been recorded by the chronicle. His face was publicly exposed for what witnesses described as the rare ceremonial moment of his hood removal during the [[Nohlle]] address. The chronicle records [[Trinity Stolen|Trinity]]'s private characterization of him: *"That manipulative old coot."*

The Gheronda is **the same man as [[The Cardinal]]** — the Hypostasis title and the public-Church title are two operational identities of a single supreme authority. The Cardinal's *"Absent Authority"* status at the public Church reflects the fact that the man holding the title spent most of his time at the Hypostasis HQ in the west rather than at the public Church's centre. This dual identity is what allows him to survive the late-Fracture detonation that annihilates the rest of the Church's high doctrine: he was not physically present at the central ritual because his real seat was elsewhere.
