# Valham

A small town located two forests from [[Sahlen]]. By contemporary reputation, Valham was a place of criminals and poverty, where the law of the larger settlements thinned and the work of those who preferred not to be observed could be conducted with relative ease.

## Strategic Use

The very qualities that made Valham unattractive to ordinary travellers made it useful to those who needed to disappear. [[Hypostasis Monastery]] maintained a contact at the inn of its most secluded quarter — [[Lauren Fern]], at the time of the contract — and used the town as a safe-haven node for agents passing into and out of central Mehrendt.

[[Trinity Stolen]] retreated to Valham within minutes of leaving Ezericht's house on the night of the South Gate raid, covering her tracks at speed to ensure no pursuit could follow.
