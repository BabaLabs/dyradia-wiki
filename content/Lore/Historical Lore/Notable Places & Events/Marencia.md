# Marencia

A polity adjacent to [[Mehrendt]] in the period preceding [[The Fracture]], referenced by the Gheronda in the [[Nohlle]] balcony address as *"the lands of Marencia"* and identified as the destination to which the royal family of Mehrendt had been sent for protection following the King's reported abduction.

## The Reported Deaths

The Gheronda's public account placed the **prince and princess** of Mehrendt — the legitimate heirs whose continued existence had previously blocked the Church's contract on King [[Alaric La'Monte|Alaric]] — at Marencia at the time of their reported deaths. He claimed that a messenger had been dispatched to instruct the royal family to remain at Marencia *for protection,* and that the messenger had arrived *too late* — that the family had been *attacked and killed by the rebels on the way back* before the message reached them. The chronicle, in the absence of other testimony, attributes the deaths to the Gheronda's own agents under rebel-band cover. See [[Chapter 12 - The Coronation of Lies]].
