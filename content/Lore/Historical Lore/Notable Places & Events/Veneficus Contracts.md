# The Veneficus Contracts

A magical binding instrument of the [[Hypostasis Monastery]] tradition, used to formalize agreements in which the consequences of betrayal must exceed the consequences of compliance. Three variants are documented in the chronicle.

## The Three Forms

### Veneficuscruor-pactum *(Blood Contract)*

Signed in the blood of all parties, including the agents of the monastery. The contract binds not in honour but in *being*: violation results in the violator's existence being erased from the world — *signature and person both* — by means the monastery does not publicly explain.

### Veneficuscoul-pactum *(Soul Contract)*

The same binding consequences as the blood form, but signed against the soul rather than the blood. Used in cases where a signatory either cannot or will not offer blood (e.g. those with no blood of their own, or those whose profession depends on never surrendering it). The soul version is structurally equivalent to the blood version in its enforcement.

### Certamen-pactum *(Combined Contract)*

A single contract that combines blood and soul signatures across different signatories, so that all parties to a multi-party agreement sign exactly once and the consequences bind uniformly. The certamen form is used when a party-set is mixed — some signing in blood, others in soul.

## Properties

- **Limbo enforcement.** Those who cannot die in ordinary ways do not escape veneficus enforcement. Such violators are drawn into a limbo dimension and held there indefinitely.
- **Self-burning.** The contract destroys itself when its purpose is completed and the terms have been satisfied, releasing all parties from its binding.
- **Memory neutrality.** The veneficus instrument does not, of itself, alter memory. Memory-related provisions must be negotiated separately and are typically kept under different ownership than the mission contract itself.

## First Documented Use in the Chronicle

The contract negotiated by [[Trinity Stolen]] at [[Sahlen]] on the night of the South Gate raid, binding [[Ezericht La'Monte]], [[Edward Z Zwarf]], [[Six]], and [[Cain]] to the rescue mission for the missing King — [[Alaric La'Monte]] — at the agreed price of 2,500 gold each plus a 500-gold group outfitting bonus. See [[Chapter 3 - The Contract and the Camp]].

## The Three Sahlen Instruments

The full text of the contracts drafted by the Hypostasis Gheronda and delivered to [[Trinity Stolen]] for the Sahlen signing — as read aloud at the signing — comprised three documents:

### Absentis Consensio *(Mission Agreement)*

A *certamen-partum* combining blood and soul instruments into a single binding. Twelve substantive clauses governing the agreement among signatories regarding the safe being of Dyradia's current King. Defined: the *Mission*, the *Monastery* (represented by Gheronda and Trinity Stolen), and the *Reward* (2,500 gold coins per signatory at completion). Conditions of agreement included silence outside the contract, disclosure of new mission-relevant information to the monastery (and reciprocal disclosure from the monastery), the *answer-on-demand* clause (any signatory's relevant question to any other signatory must be answered), and the rule that the King's safe being could not be put at risk by any signatory. Clauses 10–12 specified handling of signatory death, late additions, and contract nullification on the King's death.

### Crour Consensio *(Blood Agreement)*

A single-clause instrument binding [[Six]], after signing, never to alter or use [[Trinity Stolen]]'s blood in any form. Consequence equivalent to the larger instrument.

### Memoria Consensio *(Memory Agreement)*

A single-clause instrument binding any member of the [[Hypostasis Monastery|monastery]] from altering [[Edward Z Zwarf|Edward]]'s memory in any form — deletion, replacement, or other — at any time after signing. Recorded under the spelling **Edward Zywarf** as preserved from Edward's own self-introduction at the negotiation, before the Mehrendian shortened-form **Zwarf** became his canonical name. The memory contract was kept under Edward's personal custody. See [[Chapter 5 - Of Cat-Eyes and Contracts]].
