# Stillvein

A muscle-quieting tincture compounded and named by [[Ezericht La'Monte]] of his own work as an herbist. Stillvein calms the muscles of the hands and arms, suppressing tremor and shake, and is used principally to steady the hand for difficult or long-range archery.

## Effect

The drinker experiences a brief cold chill across the body upon swallowing, followed by an unusual fluidity and steadiness of motion that lasts for a working window of several minutes. The tincture pairs well with magical vision-enhancement and other targeting aids; [[Cain]] used it to compound with his distance-vision incantation during the dawn ambush of Ezericht's house.

## Composition

Not recorded in the chronicle. Ezericht described himself as the inventor and named the preparation himself; the recipe is held privately in his cabinet of dark-glass vials.

## First Documented Use

During the dawn ambush of Ezericht's [[Sahlen]] house, Ezericht offered the vial to [[Cain]] to enable a sniper-killing shot at long range from a cramped window. The shot succeeded. See [[Chapter 4 - Of Stones and Stillvein]].
