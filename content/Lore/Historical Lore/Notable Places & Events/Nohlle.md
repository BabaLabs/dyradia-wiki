# Nohlle

The capital city of [[Mehrendt]] in the period immediately preceding [[The Fracture]] — the seat of the legitimate royal court, the site of the [[Hypostasis Monastery]]'s primary public-facing outpost, and the stage of the Gheronda's foundational propaganda address at the close of the events recorded in [[Chapter 12 - The Coronation of Lies]].

## Civic Geography

The chronicle records, of Nohlle's principal civic features:

- A **central plaza** large enough to gather the city's full attendance for a high-monastic address.
- A **wide canal** at the plaza's western margin separating the patio district from the streets of the lower city.
- A **harbour** visible from the high narrow windows of the monastery's audience chambers — suggesting Nohlle was a coastal capital.
- The [[Hypostasis Monastery]]'s **capital outpost** at the plaza's northern margin, with a balcony from which public addresses were delivered.

## The Coronation of Lies

The Gheronda's foundational public address from the Hypostasis balcony at Nohlle — announcing the King's death, blaming the *rebel militia*, denouncing the bastard heir, declaring the simultaneous death of the rest of the royal family in their return from [[Marencia]], and asking the people for their loyalty in the war he had just declared — was delivered to the assembled citizens of Nohlle on the evening of the regicide. See [[Chapter 12 - The Coronation of Lies]].

The kneeling of the assembled crowd at the Gheronda's removal of his hood is preserved as the chronicle's principal documentation of the rare public exposure of the Gheronda's face.

## The Fall of Nohlle (Multi-Stage)

Nohlle did not survive the Fracture as a continuous city. Its loss occurred in stages over the years following the events of [[The Origin Chronicle/index|the Origin Chronicle]]'s Part One:

### Stage 1 — The District ([[Edward Z Zwarf|Z]]'s Awakening)

Within the first few years after the regicide, [[Edward Z Zwarf|Edward Z Zwarf]] — the Mehrendt Commander whose [[The Awoken|Awoken]] fire-affinity had been masked since his first commission by an issued turquoise amulet — broke under extreme emotional duress when the amulet's protection failed. Z's first uncontrolled Awakening burned a district of Nohlle off the map. The Church, which had been preparing the public for an anti-magic war for the better part of two decades (the **Holy Crusades** of approximately twenty years prior having been its public conditioning phase), did not need to manufacture the resulting outrage. They had been ready. They declared the crusade against magic and the Awoken formally. See [[Chapter 11 - The Vanished Friend]] for Ezericht's first canonical mention of the Holy Crusades; see [[The Awoken]] and [[Edward Z Zwarf]] for the Awakening event itself.

### Stage 2 — The War

The propaganda war the Cardinal/Gheronda declared from the balcony became a real war over the following decades. Nohlle, as both the public seat of the Church and the visible site of the founding incident, was repeatedly damaged by the conflict. The city did not fall in a single blow; it was eroded across multiple stages of the early Fracture conflict.

### Stage 3 — The Detonation

The late-Fracture climax (~30-41 AF) — the controlled magical detonation by which [[Maeleus]] and [[Ometrieysa]] annihilated the Church's high doctrine and prevented the cataclysmic ritual the Church had been preparing — occurred *in what remained of Nohlle.* The detonation finished much of what the war had begun. The [[The Cardinal|Cardinal]] survived because his real seat was at the [[Hypostasis Monastery]] HQ in the western borders rather than the Mehrendt centre.

### Stage 4 — Abandonment

The remnants of Nohlle were abandoned over the years following the Severing (41 AF). The city was not razed to the foundations; it was *left.* The unrepaired districts were colonized by the new growth of the Stabilization era, and the unrepaired civic structures fell into the slow ruin of unattended stone. By the time [[Valemarch]] was rebuilt as Mehrendt's new capital in the early Stabilization (~50-100 AF), Nohlle had ceased to function as a city in any administrative sense.

### Status at Campaign Present (327 AF)

Nohlle's footprint remains visible in the geographic record. The exact present-day status of the site is reserved for chronicle work yet to come.
