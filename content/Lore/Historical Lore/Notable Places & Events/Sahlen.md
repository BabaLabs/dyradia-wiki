# Sahlen

A fortified Mehrendt city of the pre-Fracture and early-Fracture years. Known to have hosted repeated military incursions in its final decades — raids that drew increasingly capable defenders to its walls and gates.

## Geography & Layout

What little survives of Sahlen's civic record describes a walled urban settlement of moderate size, with named gates by cardinal direction. The **South Gate** was a known defensive choke point and the site of one of the city's more notable engagements. The city held dense alleyways, roof-traversable buildings, and at least one tavern of repute frequented by long-resident patrons.

## Role in the Origin Chronicle

Sahlen is the city in which [[Ezericht La'Monte]], [[Edward Z Zwarf]], and the necromancer [[Six]] first met — during a raid on the South Gate on the anniversary of Ezericht's adopted family's death. See [[Chapter 1 - The Anniversary Raid]].

The second wave of that night's attack revealed that **the city's South Gate had been opened from the inside by a member of Sahlen's own guard.** The identity of the guard was, at the time, unknown. See [[Chapter 2 - The Monk and the Mission]].
