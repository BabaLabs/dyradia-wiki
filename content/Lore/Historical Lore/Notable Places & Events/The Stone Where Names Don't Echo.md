# The Stone Where Names Don't Echo

A hidden monument deep within [[Gohl-Khazir]] / Stonehome. Commemorates the sacrifice of [[Maeleus]] and [[Ometrieysa]] — though by design, the monument does not name them.

---

## Description

A sealed alcove with no sigils. No plaque. Just a carved basin containing cooled glass — the result of heat so intense it liquified stone.

If a player touches the basin:

- The stone hums like a heartbeat
- The air smells faintly of ozone
- A single arc of white-silver light flickers

Etched **inside** the basin, only visible at the right angle:

> **"We were meant to live.**
> **We chose to save instead."**

## Why Gohl-Khazir?

The placement is deliberate. Dwarven culture treats recorded truth as sacred and historical revisionism as a profound moral failing. If anywhere in Dyradia would preserve the memory of [[Maeleus]] and [[Ometrieysa]]'s sacrifice without telling the world they remember — it would be here.

The monument honors a truth the world refuses to accept.
