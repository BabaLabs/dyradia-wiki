# Dunmisk

A small town in the western part of Dyradia, of moderate poverty and immoderate criminality. The chronicle understands the [[Hypostasis Monastery]] to have maintained an undercover network of approximately five agents in the town at the period of the Origin Chronicle's principal events, kept in reserve against need.

## Significance to the Chronicle

[[Trinity Stolen]] travelled to Dunmisk by means of an order-issued translocation paper after the dispersal of the company from [[Sahlen]], and overheard, in the tavern at the town's northern margin, the first canonical reference in the chronicle to the **Dark Harbingers** cult — including the rumour that the cult's leader knew the King's whereabouts and that the cult had moved to the west.

See [[Chapter 9 - The Hour the World Turned]].
