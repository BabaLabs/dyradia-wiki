# Cain

An assassin of significant reputation across Mehrendt in the years surrounding [[The Fracture]]. Known to operate by contract, alone, with a high completion rate and a notoriously cautious approach to professional secrecy.

## Manner & Capabilities

- **Bow and twin daggers.** Carried a bow strung short for compact draw and an arrow stash in a back-pocket arrangement of his coat (he disliked quivers). Wore daggers on both hips, including an ebony-bladed dagger on his left.
- **Whispered magic.** Used a small set of command-phrases of unknown linguistic origin to extend his senses and abilities — among them: *Lumshackl strurk shiltan* for sharpened night-distance vision; *Shmick sitan* to release the same; *Themial Snatos* for amplified hearing of distinct sources; *donai remiols* for sustained overland speed; *ugth remials* for shadow-assisted disappearance.
- **Contracts.** Worked for the highest bidder, openly. His professional reputation was the only collateral he offered, and he protected it.

## Background

Cain killed a giant prince in earlier years, on contract. He has identified this as the precipitating event that caused the giants of the northern ranges to withdraw from Mehrendt and from association with humans. He remembered the road to their camp; he had reason to.

He carries an unspecified trauma involving a child — when an ordinary child running through the streets of [[Sahlen]] brushed against him, the contact triggered a brief flashback of another child, in another place, in arms-outstretched terror. He let the present-day child go. The chronicle does not record what the past child had to do with him.

## Known Engagements

- **The South Gate of [[Sahlen]].** Provided intelligence to [[Edward Z Zwarf]] on the approaching squad before the engagement began. Did not himself fight in either wave.
- **A Sahlen contract.** Completed a private contract on an unfaithful husband on the night of the second wave, fulfilled by a single bow-shot through an open window.
- **The Hypostasis Recruitment.** Summoned by Edward Z Zwarf to the Sahlen house meeting; signed the **veneficuscoul-pactum** rather than the blood version, on the grounds that *"no one gets my blood."*
- **The Northern Giant Camp.** Rode to the camp at speed on the night of recruitment and discovered the entire non-combatant population slaughtered, drawing the conclusion that the Sahlen raid had been a diversion to leave the camp undefended. See [[Chapter 3 - The Contract and the Camp]].
