# 📜 **The Awoken**

_Also known as: The Attuned, The Resonant, The Conduits_

---

## **Overview**

**Classification:** Historical Magical Phenomenon  
**First Recorded Emergence:** Late Pre-Fracture Era  
**Last Confirmed Appearance:** During the Fracture  
**Current Status:** Extinct (Presumed)

The **Awoken** were rare individuals capable of drawing power directly from elemental and planar resonance without the mediation of spellcraft, divine intercession, or ritual structure. Unlike conventional spellcasters, the Awoken did not _cast_ magic — they **expressed** it.

Their existence remains one of the most controversial and poorly understood aspects of Dyradian history.

---

## **Origin**

The Awoken lineage traces to [[Bohrova]]'s union with a Dyra attuned to the energy of the spirit. Through their rumored hybrid child, humans attuned to the energies of the Dyra were introduced to the world.

---

## **Defining Characteristics**

Accounts of the Awoken consistently describe the following traits:

- Direct attunement to a singular elemental resonance
    
- Magical output exceeding contemporary spellcasting norms
    
- Absence of verbal, somatic, or material components
    
- Heightened emotional-magic feedback loops
    
- Severe psychological and physiological strain
    

Awoken abilities were neither teachable nor transferable. Attunement was singular, involuntary, and irreversible.

## **Tiered Knowledge Accounts**

---

### **Tier 0 — Common Knowledge**

_(No roll required)_

> _“The Awoken were living weapons. People who let magic take over their bodies until they lost control. That’s why they don’t exist anymore.”_

Most people believe:

- The Awoken were dangerous
    
- They caused the Fracture
    
- They were proof magic could not be trusted
    
- Their disappearance was necessary
    

This narrative is widespread — and deliberately simple.

---

### **Tier 1 — Familiar History**

_(Low DC History, Arcane or Military Background)_

> _“The Awoken were individuals born with a natural attunement to elemental forces. While powerful, they were unstable and often suffered from emotional volatility.”_

At this level, it is known that:

- Awoken were rare
    
- They were tied to elemental forces
    
- Many served as defenders or champions
    
- Their power came at great personal cost
    

Educational texts emphasize _risk_ over _context_.

---

### **Tier 2 — Archival / Scholarly Record**

_(Moderate–High DC History, Feythran or Gohlstout Sources)_

> _“Primary records indicate the Awoken functioned as direct conduits between Dyradia and deeper planar resonance, bypassing the stabilizing frameworks later imposed on magic.”_

Scholars agree:

- The Awoken did not draw power _from_ the Dyra — but _through_ the same stabilizing structures
    
- Their abilities intensified under emotional stress
    
- Prolonged use caused neural, spiritual, and somatic degradation
    
- They were not inherently violent
    

Critical finding:

> The Awoken were **not unstable by nature**.  
> They were _unsupported_ by a world unprepared for them.

This reframes their “volatility” as systemic failure.

---

### **Tier 3 — Obscure / Suppressed Knowledge**

_(High DC, Elven Oral Record, Restricted Archives)_

> _“Elven testimony describes the Awoken as transitional beings — mortals whose existence was only possible while certain planar alignments remained intact.”_

Deep records suggest:

- Awoken emergence correlated with Interstitial integrity
    
- Their attunement required Dyra-mediated coherence
    
- The Fracture rendered such attunement impossible
    

Most disturbingly:

- At least one Awoken survived long enough to _sense_ the severance
    
- Their final manifestations were exponentially destructive
    
- Records imply awareness — not madness
    

A particularly restricted account states:

> _“He did not lose control.  
> He was screaming because the world no longer fit him.”_

---

## **The Awoken and the Fracture**

The destruction of part of Mehrendt’s capital is widely attributed to an Awoken manifestation during the Fracture. This event became the defining image of Awoken existence in public memory.

Archival reinterpretations suggest:

- The manifestation occurred under extreme emotional trauma
    
- The Awoken involved had recently experienced forced suppression
    
- The resulting catastrophe coincided with planar destabilization
    

This moment became the **justification** for the Church’s final crusade against uncontrolled magic.

---

## **Disappearance**

Following the Fracture:

- No new Awoken have been documented
    
- Known survivors vanished or perished
    
- Attempts to replicate attunement failed catastrophically
    

Modern consensus holds that:

- The conditions necessary for Awakening no longer exist
    

A minority theory posits:

> The world itself now rejects that state of being.

## **Cultural Memory**

### **Mehrendt**

- Remembers the Awoken as symbols of catastrophe
    
- Uses them to justify magical regulation
    

### **Feythra**

- Mourns the Awoken as lost bridges between worlds
    
- Considers their extinction a tragedy
    

### **Gohlstout**

- Records Awoken manifestations clinically
    
- Notes correlation between Awoken absence and material anomalies
    

### **Avalon**

- Reveres the Awoken as proof of mortal potential
    
- Considers their eradication an unforgivable crime

## **Modern Implications**

Although no Awoken exist today:

- Certain magical anomalies mirror Awoken resonance
    
- Artifacts occasionally respond as if to attunement
    
- Unstable regions exhibit Awoken-like effects
    

There is growing concern among scholars that:

> Reconnection of severed planar structures may permit  
> **something like the Awoken to exist again — imperfectly.**

## **Named Awoken — Origin Chronicle**

The chronicle preserves the identities of two Awoken from the Fracture-era who are otherwise referenced in this file by description only.

### [[Edward Z Zwarf]] — *The Firebound Commander*

The Awoken of fire affinity whose uncontrolled Awakening destroyed a district of the pre-Fracture capital [[Nohlle]] — the event the Tier 2 archival record attributes to *"the destruction of part of Mehrendt's capital"* and the Tier 3 obscure account documents in the line *"He did not lose control. He was screaming because the world no longer fit him."* The Mehrendt military had masked his Awoken signature for years by means of a **turquoise amulet** issued at his first commission. The Awakening surfaced when the amulet's protection failed under extreme emotional duress in the months following the regicide of King [[Alaric La'Monte]].

The Church had been preparing the public for an anti-magic war for the better part of two decades — the [[#Holy Crusades]] of approximately twenty years prior were the public conditioning phase. Edward's Awakening provided the incident. The resulting Church crusade against magic and the Awoken became the founding event of the Fracture's public war.

See [[Chapter 6 - The Amulet and the Dagger]] for the amulet's first canonical chronicle appearance; subsequent chapters track its function.

### [[Ometrieysa]] — *The Weapon Who Remembered Herself*

The Awoken broken and rebuilt by the [[Church of Prim]] as their holy weapon. Her Awakening — Dyric energy, lightning, spirit — surged beyond containment in the final battle of the late-Fracture climax. Anchored by [[Maeleus]] in the controlled detonation that ended both their lives, her Awakening served as the instrument that annihilated the Church's high doctrine and prevented the cataclysmic ritual the Church had been preparing.

Her case is the chronicle's clearest demonstration of the Awoken's "Dyric energy" tether — the magical inheritance of [[Bohrova]]'s union with the [[The Dyra|Dyra]] expressing itself through a body the Church had forcibly reshaped.

See [[Ometrieysa]] and [[Maeleus]] for the full canonical arc.
