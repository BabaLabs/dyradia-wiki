# Zant

A travelling monk of elvish features, in [[Sahlen]] on the night of the South Gate raid by his own account for *"reasons of [his] own"* — reasons he declined to specify when pressed.

## Manner & Capabilities

- **Travelling monk.** Carried an old book of chants at his hip and a staff that proved to be more than ornamental.
- **Healer.** Could chant a wounded man's bleeding to stillness and his wounds to closure. Used this to keep a one-armed prisoner alive at [[Edward Z Zwarf|Edward]]'s direction, and later to mend Edward's own wounds in the second skirmish.
- **Weatherworking.** Drew rain from a clear sky to extinguish the fires burning across Sahlen, and called a bolt of lightning down on a charging squad with a gesture.
- **Fire-staff.** His staff opened at the tip on command to release a column of fire capable of incinerating several infantrymen at a stroke.

## First Documented Appearance

Entered the tavern at [[Sahlen]] after the South Gate engagement, ordered two pints, and seated himself within earshot of the company. Joined the second wave of the raid at [[Edward Z Zwarf|Edward]]'s order — under protest at the manner of the order — and brought down the storm that ended the fires.

See [[Chapter 1 - The Anniversary Raid]] for his arrival and [[Chapter 2 - The Monk and the Mission]] for his involvement in the second engagement.

## The True Reason for Sahlen

Zant had come to Sahlen for an artifact: a purple stone he had located in the upper room of an abandoned house on the city's eastern margin, in the possession of two ragged squatters who had only recently discovered it. He recovered it the same night, burning the house, paralyzing the squatters, and leaving Sahlen by the town gate. The stone's nature, origin, and intended use are not yet specified in the chronicle. See [[Chapter 4 - Of Stones and Stillvein]].

He was recruited the same morning by [[Trinity Stolen]] for the Hypostasis rescue mission, accepting both her offer and (after some initial refusal) her offered reward of 2,500 gold coins.

## The Stone's Function

The purple stone proved, when Zant took receipt of his eastern territory in Trinity's chain-and-bracelet division, to be a teleportation focus: held in open palm and asked, it carried its bearer across distance in a single burst of white light. Zant used it to cross from [[Sahlen]] to an unnamed eastern village in the small hour after the company's dispersal, where night still held while morning had broken in Sahlen.

## The Loss to the Imp

In the deserted inn of the eastern village, Zant encountered a small dark-red winged creature — the chronicle for lack of a better term calls it an *imp* — which closed the corridor between them in a single bound, brushed against his coat, and was gone in a small puff of sulphurous smoke. The pouch containing the purple stone went with it. Zant's recovery of the stone is, at the time the chronicle takes its first record of the eastern village, an open matter.

See [[Chapter 7 - Of Stones and Brothers]] for the teleportation and the loss.
