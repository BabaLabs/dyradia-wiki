# Ometrieysa

*The Weapon Who Remembered Herself*

---

## Overview (Player-Safe)

Ometrieysa is remembered as a high-ranking Church leader and rumored arcane prodigy. Some legends insist she performed miracles; others accuse her of horrors equal to any inquisitor.

Her death coincides with the collapse of the [[Church of Prim]]'s leadership.

## Tier 3 — Forbidden Truth

She and [[Maeleus]] once loved each other in secret before the war.

She broke her own conditioning for minutes — just long enough to remember him. Just long enough to choose her own death on her terms. Just long enough to destroy the ritual they were meant to complete together.

Their last act was a mutual mercy.

**Final recorded words (unverified):**

> *"It was never holiness. It was hunger."*

## Backstory — Before the Weapon

Ometrieysa (birth name TBD) was born in Avalon to a lower-ranking member of the Avalonian echelons. Her parents and her culture were firmly against anything gender non-conforming or non-heteronormative, and tried on many occasions to convince her she was mistaken about herself.

She and [[Maeleus]] fell in love in secret as teenagers. Their love was quiet, stolen, desperate in the way doomed things are.

When she first confided, *"I am not who they say I am,"* Maeleus answered:

> *"Then come where I can love you honestly."*

He begged her to elope to [[Mehrendt]], where she could transition with no judgment. But her father, falling under the influence of lower-ranking Church members, instead handed her over to "re-education."

When the [[Church of Prim|Church]] discovered her connection to Maeleus and the Avalonian echelons, they made a different choice than her family had wanted: rather than "correct" her gender, they enabled her transition — *and weaponized it as leverage*. They brainwashed her, indoctrinated her, transitioned her, empowered her — built her into the perfect holy executioner.

Maeleus was told she killed herself to escape the Church's "corrective doctrine." He carried that grave like a wedding band.

---

## What She Became

Inside the Church, Ometrieysa did not die — she was *rebuilt*. Brainwashed, indoctrinated, empowered, transitioned — weaponized into the perfect holy executioner.

She became **Grand Priestess Ometrieysa**, the Church's arcane prodigy. A living blade of doctrine. A miracle the Church claimed as its own.

When she and Maeleus first faced each other on the battlefield, neither knew what to do with the recognition that passed between them. The way she held a blade like it was the only truthful thing in her life. The way her voice cracked, just barely, on his name.

---

## The End — Mutual Mercy

In their final battle, the brainwashing slipped and she remembered. Not everything. Not clearly. But enough.

Enough to hate what she'd become. Enough to reach for the person she once loved.

> **"You were supposed to save me."**
> **"I did."**
> **"Then why didn't you take me with you?"**
> **"…Because they would've only taken you again."**

Her body became unstable — magically, not metaphorically. Her awakening surged beyond containment: lightning, spirit, raw Dyric energy clawing through the seams of reality.

If she had died uncontrolled, she would have torn open a rift that finishes what the [[The Fracture|Fracture]] later becomes.

She begged Maeleus to kill her. He refused. So she made the choice for both of them.

She drew the power inward. He held her — not to stop her, but to anchor her. They became the eye of their own storm. The explosion annihilated high-ranking Church leadership, collapsed a ritual that could have shattered Dyradia entirely, and bought [[Ezericht La'Monte]] and the others time to survive.

They died together.

History erased her the moment it finished using her.
