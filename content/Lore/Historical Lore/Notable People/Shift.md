# Shift

A small creature in the keeping of [[Trinity Stolen]] of the [[Hypostasis Monastery]].

## Manner & Capabilities

- **Shapeshifting.** Capable of taking the form of a necklace ornament (its default carried form, worn by Trinity) and of shifting into a form so small as to be effectively unfindable in the open air — *"smaller than a fly, making it impossible to perceive."*
- **Courier function.** Could carry whispered instruction from its keeper to a recipient at significant distance, suggesting either trained flight, magical conveyance, or a faculty the chronicle has not yet clarified.
- **Loyalty.** Acted on Trinity's voice without hesitation or apparent need for further instruction.

## Documented Use

Sent by [[Trinity Stolen]] from the top of [[Sahlen]]'s north tower to deliver a report to Trinity's **Gheronda** (her order's elder), confirming the identification of viable recruits among the company at the South Gate engagement. See [[Chapter 2 - The Monk and the Mission]].
