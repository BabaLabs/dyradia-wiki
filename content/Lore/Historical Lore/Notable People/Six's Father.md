# Six's Father

The patriarchal figure to whom [[Six]] and his four brothers — [[Natas]], [[Marxus]], [[Nazu]], and [[Zin]] — return when their travels permit. The chronicle has not yet preserved his proper name.

## The Castle in the Swamp

The father keeps a long, partially-ruined castle deep in a swamp in the south of Dyradia. The structure has been, at some point in its history, a battlefield; it has not since been repaired, and the moss, vines, and standing water of the surrounding wetland have made significant progress on its lower halls. The father lives in the structure year-round.

## Manner

A small, slightly bent old man with white brows and a faint red colour to his eyes that resembles, in the morning light, the same colour [[Six]]'s eyes take on after he has drawn blood at sufficient quantity. He laughs heartily and speaks briefly. The chronicle records him as patient, watchful, and not particularly forthcoming about the circumstances of his sons' first coming-into-being.

## Capabilities

- **Bone-switch.** Set into the long low table of his hall is a small unobtrusive bone switch of his own making, by which he can cause the bracelets worn by his sons to pulse — singly or in combination — to call them home.
- **Pocket-watch teleport devices.** The father is the maker of the small pocket-watch contraptions each of his sons carries. The devices teleport their bearer to the castle by pressing a single central button. The chronicle understands the devices were issued at the time each son was first *"born."*

## Documented Action

Called all four of his unoccupied sons (Natas, Marxus, Nazu, Zin) home shortly after [[Six]]'s arrival at the castle and dispatched them across Dyradia to identify and — if necessary — eliminate any party that might interfere with Six's mission for *"the Church."* See [[Chapter 7 - Of Stones and Brothers]].

## Earlier Experiments

The chronicle records, on Six's first canonical disclosure of the matter, that the technique by which Six and his elemental brothers were produced was **not the technique's first iteration**. Six's father had experimented on many, many people before perfecting the process that produced his five surviving sons.

At least one such earlier experiment produced a being of Six's kind who — at some point in his life — *chose mortality back*: drained his own soul-stone's binding by his own act, gave the residue-stone to a young [[Ezericht La'Monte|Ezericht]] as a parting relic, and died as an ordinary mortal does. The earlier being's name has not been preserved. He had befriended Ezericht in the years between Ezericht's foster-placement and his adulthood, and had told Ezericht: *"In the end, you'll find it out anyways."*

See [[Chapter 11 - The Vanished Friend]] for the recognition of the relic-stone by Six.
