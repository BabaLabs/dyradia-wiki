# Snap

A regular at the [[Sahlen]] Inn in the pre-Fracture years, known by sight to the barkeep and to most of the patrons of the establishment.

## Appearance & Trade

Wore ragged brown shorts and no shirt. Carried an unusually large axe across his back and a hatchet on either side of his belt. Sold trinkets in the streets of Sahlen by day; drank in the Inn by night, with the unhurried consistency of a man for whom both pursuits had long since become the shape of his life.

## Known Appearances

- **The Sahlen Inn, the night of the raid.** Helped [[Six]] reattach an arm that had been severed in an alley fight earlier that evening, holding the replacement arm — taken from the creature that had severed his original — against Six's shoulder while Six sewed it on. He concluded, with the patient logic of a sufficiently drunk man, that the entire episode was probably a weird drunk dream and that it did not, therefore, particularly matter whether he assisted. See [[Chapter 3 - The Contract and the Camp]].
- **The Doorway, the morning after.** Returning into Sahlen through the main gate with a deer he had taken before dawn, Snap encountered the wounded [[Trinity Stolen]] at [[Ezericht La'Monte|Ezericht]]'s shack-door, dropped the deer, and offered to remove the arrow or to fetch medical help. He was the only man present that morning whose first response to Trinity's altered face was to remain exactly where he was and wait for her to speak. He afterward offered [[Six]] the deer's blood for ritual use, which Six declined. See [[Chapter 5 - Of Cat-Eyes and Contracts]].

## Hunting Practice

Snap hunted before dawn with a longbow and worked the carcasses in side-alleys with his hatchets. The hatchets at his belt were habitually blood-stained from the morning's work and he did not appear to consider this a thing in need of cleaning before midday.
