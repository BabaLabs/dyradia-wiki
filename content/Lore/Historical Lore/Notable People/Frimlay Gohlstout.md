# 📜 **Frimlay Gohlstout**

_Keeper of the Deep Record_  
_(Gohlstout)_

---

## **Overview**

**Titles:** King of Gohlstout  
**Status:** Deceased

Frimlay Gohlstout is remembered as a genial ruler whose love of lore masked a formidable intellect. Under his reign, Gohlstout became a refuge for scholars and preserved truths others destroyed.

---

### **Tier 0 — Common Knowledge**

> _“A good king. Loved ale. Loved stories.”_

---

### **Tier 1 — Familiar History**

> _“Frimlay protected ancient knowledge during the Fracture.”_

Known facts:

- He denied Church access to dwarven archives
    
- He sheltered refugees
    
- He remained militarily neutral
    

---

### **Tier 2 — Archival Record**

> _“Frimlay ordered the sealing of multiple vaults following the Fracture.”_

Records show:

- Entire libraries were removed from circulation
    
- Some records remain unopened
    
- The seals were never meant to be permanent
    

---

### **Tier 3 — Obscure Knowledge**

> _“Frimlay believed the world would need the truth later.”_

He did not believe it could survive it then.
