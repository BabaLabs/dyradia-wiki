# Edward Z Zwarf

*The Firebound Commander.* Known in the field as **Z**, and to his charges as **Edward**. A protector by trade in his earliest known years, and later a commander of consequence in the Fracture-era conflicts.

## Origins & Trade

Edward Zwarf surfaced in the historical record as a hired protector — paid, by his own statement, *"quite a shiny coin"* by a patron he did not name and did not particularly care to identify. His charge for one period of his early career was [[Ezericht La'Monte]], whom Edward shadowed for an entire month before introducing himself, choosing the night of a raid on [[Sahlen]] to do so.

He approached his protective work as a methodical professional. He knew the alleyways and rooftops of his charge's city before he ever spoke to him. He kept information rather than hoarding it.

## Military Identity

Edward's bodyguard-for-coin presentation was a cover identity. His true position was **Commander Zywarf** of the Mehrendt military, operating under the senior authority of [[General Flitney]] of the [[Sahlen]] armory. His protective work for [[Ezericht La'Monte]] was a long-running military programme to safeguard the unrecognized bastard son of King [[Alaric La'Monte]] until such time as Ezericht could be used to perform the act the military's last-resort plan required of him.

The military preserved the original Mehrendian-formal surname spelling **Zywarf** in its official records (e.g. the Memoria Consensio memory contract signed at Sahlen, registered to *Edward Zywarf*); the contracted form **Zwarf** developed as the common-tongue shortening Edward used in field work where the formal spelling was either inconvenient or revealing.

See [[Chapter 6 - The Amulet and the Dagger]] for the disclosure of his military identity to [[Ezericht La'Monte|Ezericht]].

## Manner & Capabilities

- **Broadsword and bow.** Equally fluent with both. Capable of unstringing his bow and drawing his blade in a single motion, and of dropping from a wall-top into a melee without breaking the rhythm of either.
- **Tactical.** Issued clear orders under pressure. At Sahlen's South Gate he directed Ezericht to the squad leader and assigned the necromancer who'd appeared from nowhere to support him on the line — without pause, without consultation, and correctly.
- **Information-driven.** Cultivated contacts. At Sahlen, a man named **Cain** had informed him in advance of the squad approaching from the south. He valued such relationships and protected them by discretion.
- **Pragmatic regarding necromancy.** When [[Six]] joined the South Gate defense by raising the dead, Edward did not refuse the help — but he was clear, immediately and aloud, that he would not distinguish thralls from the living people they had been if they stepped into his line of attack.
- **Drinks rapidly and without ceremony.** Drained his first pint at the corner table in a single breath. Took the seat with its back to the wall.

## Known Engagements

- **The South Gate of [[Sahlen]].** First documented meeting with [[Ezericht La'Monte]] and [[Six]]. See [[Chapter 1 - The Anniversary Raid]].
- **The Tavern Door at the second wave.** Direction of the storm-and-blade defense alongside [[Zant]] and [[Six]]. See [[Chapter 2 - The Monk and the Mission]].
- **The Veneficus Recruitment.** Negotiated the price upward to 2,500 gold each; demanded a separate memory contract preserved under his own keeping. See [[Chapter 3 - The Contract and the Camp]].
- **The Doorway Insult.** Took a warning shot at [[Trinity Stolen]]'s lower leg outside [[Ezericht La'Monte|Ezericht]]'s shack after the priest insulted his professionalism, and tripled his agreed fee for the resulting trouble. See [[Chapter 5 - Of Cat-Eyes and Contracts]].

## Notable Possessions

- **Turquoise amulet.** Edward kept a turquoise amulet on a cord, recovered from the bottom of his gear-bundle and worn for the day of the contract signing. The amulet's properties are not specified in the chronicle; the gesture of putting it on suggested both familiarity and reservation.
- **Specialty arrows.** His quiver included shafts of several specialty patterns — razor-tipped (for close-range cutting), eyelet-shafts (for tying lines), and shafts with small wax-sealed packages affixed at the tip (unspecified function).
- **Door-traps.** Customary practice of setting a simple line-and-weight trap inside his Inn-room door before leaving, disarmed by touch from the correct side.

## The Awoken Nature

Edward Z Zwarf is, by his bloodline, an **[[The Awoken|Awoken]]** of fire affinity. He did not know it during the events of [[The Origin Chronicle/index|the Origin Chronicle]]'s Part One. The Mehrendt military knew it. [[General Flitney]] knew it. His operational handlers knew it. Edward was never told — both to preserve his operational efficacy (an Awoken who knew himself one would have been a different commander) and to maintain the [[Church of Prim|Church]]'s deniability about his identity, which was being actively masked by issued equipment.

### The Turquoise Amulet

The **turquoise amulet** Edward wore — issued to him at his first commission, and given to [[Ezericht La'Monte|Ezericht]] from General Flitney's locked drawer on the morning of the regicide — served two purposes the bearer was not told about together:

1. **Protection against monastery contract magic** *(the publicly stated function — Edward's "bouncer")*.
2. **Masking of Awoken signature against the Church's detection instruments** *(undisclosed)*.

The Mehrendt military had been masking Edward's Awoken signature for years. The amulet was the instrument by which they did so.

### The Earning of "Firebound"

The title *Firebound Commander* was not earned in tactical command. It was earned the day Edward's fire-affinity surfaced *uncontrolled* — when his amulet either failed or was removed, and the Awoken nature he had not known he carried broke through under conditions of extreme emotional duress. The chronicle records this as the event canonically attributed in [[The Awoken]] file as *"the destruction of part of Mehrendt's capital"* — the moment that became the public justification for the Church's anti-magic crusade. The capital district that burned was a district of [[Nohlle]]. The cause was Z. The Church had been preparing the kindling for the resulting war for twenty years (see the **Holy Crusades** of approximately twenty years prior); Z provided the spark.

*Firebound* is the title earned in that act. The fire bound *him* — not the reverse. He carries the name because of what he did and because of what he could not, once he understood, take back.

The exact moment, location, and circumstance of Z's first Awakening are recorded in the chronicle of Part Two when it is written.

## Toward the Title

The Edward of Sahlen — paid to watch a man drink — would in time become a leader whose name carried the weight of his methods. The *Firebound* portion of the title would be earned later, not by command, but by failure of containment.
