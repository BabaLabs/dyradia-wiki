# Zin

The youngest of [[Six]]'s four brothers, identical to Six in face and in body, distinct in element. Zin's element is **earth**. His clothing is brown.

## Capabilities

- **Geomancy.** Zin manipulates and creates earth and stone — flowing mass across structures in waves, hardening fist to stone for impact, raising or burying landscape on command. The chronicle records that a fist of his, hardened by his element, struck an old man at a farm with sufficient force to kill on contact, after which he covered the farm under a mass of summoned earth.
- **Axe.** Zin carries an axe as his weapon of choice.
- **Soul-stone.** A soul-stone of matching brown colour, carried as an internal anchor for his elemental domain.
- **Pocket-watch teleport.** Like his brothers, Zin carries a small pocket-watch contraption made by their father which teleports him to the swamp castle on a single press of the central button.

## Temperament

The chronicle records Zin as *immensely calm* — slowest of the four to act, slowest to anger, and, when at last he does act, the most thorough. He accepted his father's recall with the unfazed patience of a man who had been called away from his cellar before and would, in time, return to it.
