# Marxus

The second of [[Six]]'s four brothers, identical to Six in face and in body, distinct in element. Marxus's element is **water**. His clothing is blue.

## Capabilities

- **Aquamancy.** Marxus manipulates and creates water in its several forms. The chronicle records him as swimming the warm currents of the southern continental shelf in long unhurried spans that did not, by his own account, require him to surface.
- **Trident.** Marxus carries a trident as his weapon of choice.
- **Soul-stone.** A soul-stone of matching blue colour, carried as an internal anchor for his elemental domain.
- **Pocket-watch teleport.** Like his brothers, Marxus carries a small pocket-watch contraption made by their father which teleports him to the swamp castle on a single press of the central button.

## Temperament

The chronicle records Marxus as *calm but easily stirred to anger* — the most outwardly composed of the brothers in ordinary circumstance, the most dangerous of them when composure failed.

## Known Engagements

- **The Monastery Gate.** Arrived at the [[Hypostasis Monastery]]'s east gate during his father's dispatch and was admitted past the bowmen on the wall by the captain's judgment that the cost of striking him exceeded the cost of letting him walk past. Drowned the gate-monk **Medinas** in a sphere of conjured water the following morning, after Medinas was the monk delegated to inform him that the mission had ended. See [[Chapter 10 - Of Gems and Fire]].
