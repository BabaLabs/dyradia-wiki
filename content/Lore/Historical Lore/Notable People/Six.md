# Six

A necromancer of unknown origin, present in [[Sahlen]] during the South Gate raid. Took the field at Ezericht La'Monte's side without first having chosen a side, by his own admission, and chose afterward to follow the company to a tavern not for the drink but to observe what came next.

## Known Practices

- **Necromancy.** Could raise the recently fallen as bound thralls; could route his thralls through living formations to multiply the count by attrition. He used this to relieve [[Ezericht La'Monte]] when Ezericht's arrogance left him outnumbered against incoming infantry.
- **Sustenance.** Carried a cross with a corked reservoir in its base from which he drew a crimson liquid for personal consumption. He used it in place of ale, dumping the contents of any cup he was given onto the floor before filling it from the cross. Whether this practice was ritual or biological necessity, no companion present in Sahlen ever determined.
- **Persistence.** Claimed, with the equanimity of one repeating a known fact rather than offering a boast, that he did not die. He declined to explain the reason.

## Manner

Six spoke sparingly and only when addressed. He cleaned his unusually long fingernails with his dagger between exchanges, and treated his necromancy as a matter of method rather than identity. When asked his name in the South Gate alley, he answered with the single word — *Six* — and turned away to find another route.

He was not, he insisted, on anyone's side until he understood what the enemy was capable of.

## Further Capabilities (Sahlen, Second Engagement)

- **Blood golem.** Six could compose, from the remains of a sufficiently large fallen enemy and a measure of supplementary blood, a constructed creature in the shape of a man-sized golem of blood and bone. He used the form once during the second wave at Sahlen.
- **Eye-change.** After drawing blood at sufficient quantity, his eyes took on a faint red tint. He wore goggles to obscure this when convenient. Neither he nor any companion of his ever explained the cause.
- **Interrogator.** Six's questioning of the captured soldier from the second wave was sustained, methodical, and conducted with the patience of a man who had done the same thing in other places to other men. The prisoner confessed that one of [[Sahlen]]'s own city guards had opened the gate from inside. See [[Chapter 2 - The Monk and the Mission]].

## The Blood-Rite

Performed at dawn the morning after the South Gate engagement. Six poured an unbroken circle of his cross's reservoir onto the floor of his Inn-room, chanted in a tongue not preserved, and caused the circle to rise from the floor and hover at his torso-height without losing its shape. He then plunged his newly-attached arm into the suspended ring; the blood entered him along his scars; the new arm took up its kinship with the rest of his body. He confirmed the rite with a clean test-cut. The cut sealed instantly. See [[Chapter 4 - Of Stones and Stillvein]].

## Blood Harvesting

Six demonstrated a practiced ability to draw fresh spilled blood off a surface — by gesture and a small glass vial — for later study. He used this technique to harvest a sample of [[Trinity Stolen]]'s blood from the spray on Ezericht's doorframe after Edward's warning shot. See [[Chapter 5 - Of Cat-Eyes and Contracts]].

## The Soul-Stone Defense

Six's soul-stone — the small dark stone he carried on a cord at the inside of his collar — was defended against unauthorized contact by a slow oily film of contact-paralytic that lived on its surface. The film passed through Six's own skin without effect and through any other's skin with the result of a paralysis that did not kill but did not, in Six's own description, deaden the body's capacity to feel: the bearer of the touch went still while continuing, with cruel completeness, to experience.

The defense's first documented activation was against [[Ezericht La'Monte|Ezericht]], who tore the stone from Six's neck on a southern ridge before Six's defensive rotation had completed. Ezericht spent the next fifteen minutes paralyzed in full body while delivering the case that secured Six's stand-down of his brother [[Zin]] from the attack at the Western Barracks. See [[Chapter 11 - The Vanished Friend]].

## Family

- [[Natas]] — fire, red, sword
- [[Marxus]] — water, blue, trident
- [[Nazu]] — wind, green, scythe
- [[Zin]] — earth, brown, axe

Each brother carries a soul-stone of matching colour and a small pocket-watch teleport device of their father's making, which returns its bearer to the family's swamp castle on a single press.

In a conversation overheard at the [[Sahlen]] Inn after his replacement-arm surgery, Six described the pocket-watch as a thing his father *"made for me when I was first 'born.'"* The audible quotation marks suggest that Six's manner of coming-into-being differs from the ordinary mortal pattern; the chronicle does not yet specify how. See [[Chapter 7 - Of Stones and Brothers]].
