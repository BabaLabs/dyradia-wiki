# General Flitney

Senior officer of the Mehrendt military in the years immediately preceding [[The Fracture]] and the operational handler of the covert military programme that placed [[Ezericht La'Monte]] with a foster family and assigned [[Edward Z Zwarf|Edward Zywarf]] to his protection.

## Position & Manner

Held the rank of General. Operated out of an office at the [[Sahlen]] town armory at the time of the chronicle's principal events, though the chronicle does not yet confirm whether Sahlen was his permanent station or a field posting connected to the King-rescue planning.

Spoke plainly. Tolerated [[Edward Z Zwarf|Edward]]'s irregular procedure with an exhausted patience that the chronicle suggests was the product of long association rather than affection — he had been corrected by Edward, on procedural matters, many times before, and had reached the considered judgment that Edward had so far been right about each of them.

## The Briefing

Conducted the Sahlen briefing in which [[Ezericht La'Monte|Ezericht]] was informed of his lineage, the nature of the Church's contract with [[Alaric La'Monte|King Alaric]], the military's hiding of the King, the propaganda war that had followed, and the military's intended solution. He authorized Ezericht's mission to assassinate the King and Edward's mission to keep Ezericht alive through it; he dispatched the **Fourth Wall Division** to the stables to provide armed escort. See [[Chapter 6 - The Amulet and the Dagger]].
