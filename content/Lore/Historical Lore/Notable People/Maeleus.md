# Maeleus

*The Knife That Held the World Together*

---

## Overview (Player-Safe)

Maeleus is remembered as a **Mehrendt general who defected to the Church**. He is widely considered a war criminal responsible for the Memorial Massacre, and believed to have died in prison.

He is a cautionary tale, a name used in lessons about betrayal.

> *"Trust is a blade with two edges. Ask the ghosts of Maeleus."* — Common proverb in [[Valemarch]]

---

## Tier 0 — Common Knowledge

*(No roll required)*

- A respected military leader who turned traitor
- Served the Church during the early Fracture
- Responsible for the slaughter of civilians at the Day of Remembrance
- Arrested and executed (or imprisoned until death — accounts vary)
- Spoken of with disgust or silence

**Truth at this tier:** he *did* commit the massacre. Nothing washes that away.

---

## Tier 1 — Familiar History

*(Scholars, veterans, clergy)*

- He was known for diplomacy more than brutality
- His betrayal was *shockingly out of character*
- Some records suggest the act was a "test of loyalty" demanded by the [[Church of Prim]]
- His body was never publicly displayed; records are sealed

**Rumor:** *some say he infiltrated the Church intentionally — but why?*

## Tier 2 — Archival Record

*(Restricted access)*

- Maeleus was conditioned from youth to become a deep-cover agent
- The massacre allowed him to be accepted into the Church's inner circle
- He attempted to assassinate members of the High Doctrine before his death
- His final days disappeared from official records entirely

**Note in margin, unsigned:**

> *"Monsters are not born. They are required."*

---

## Tier 3 — Forbidden Truth

*(Sealed Lore)*

> ⚠ This knowledge is not meant for public history.

Maeleus sacrificed his name, legacy, and soul to get close enough to [[Ometrieysa]] — the Church's arcane weapon — to stop a cataclysmic ritual. She was his lost love, taken and reforged by the Church. Their final confrontation ended in a controlled magical detonation that killed them both and decapitated Church leadership, preventing a reality tear.

He saved the world.
He will never be honored for it.

**Final documented words:**

> *"If the world must hate someone to survive, let it be me."*

## Full Arc — The Tragedy of Maeleus

### Early Life — Groomed to Fall

Maeleus of Mehrendt was born into the military like a sword is born to the forge. Trained from adolescence not merely to fight, but to *be seen*, he was intentionally sculpted into a public figure the Church could one day latch onto: charismatic, diplomatic, sympathetic to faith and reform, "reasonable" in a world going mad.

He was raised beneath the weight of a secret purpose: **to betray his country for it.**

Not truly — but convincingly. Convincingly enough to be welcomed by the Church when it needed a hero it could corrupt.

He spent his youth being admired for empathy, negotiation, mercy — all while being conditioned to someday weaponize those traits.

No one told him when the order would come. His entire life was waiting for a bell only the powerful could ring.

### Love Before the War

Before any of this mattered, there was a person — a young Avalonian who never wanted to be a soldier or a symbol. Assigned male at birth, told to be a son when they were not, [[Ometrieysa]] (birth name TBD) stood at the border between identity and punishment.

Their love was quiet, stolen, desperate in the way doomed things are.

When Ometrieysa first confided:

> *"I am not who they say I am."*

Maeleus answered:

> *"Then come where I can love you honestly."*

He begged them to run to Mehrendt. To transition where no one would own their body except them. To build a life that didn't require a war.

His father, crumbling under the [[Church of Prim|Church]]'s influence, handed her over to "corrective doctrine." They told Maeleus she killed herself to escape it.

And he believed it. He *carried that grave like a wedding band.*

### The Fall — By Necessity, Not Choice

When the Church finally approached him with false promises of power, Maeleus "hesitated" — exactly as he'd been trained to. And then he accepted.

As proof of loyalty, they demanded something unspeakable: the slaughter of unarmed civilians on a day of remembrance — because a Mehrendt official would be present.

It was a test. A cleansing. A sacrament of cruelty.

And he did it.

Not because he was broken, but because he knew the Church would never let him close enough to its heart if he refused. History remembers the event as monstrous. It was. And he chose it anyway — because infiltration demanded a devil the world would believe in.

At that moment, Maeleus died publicly. But the mission began.

### Reunion on the Battlefield

When Ometrieysa and Maeleus first faced each other on the battlefield, he recognized the stance before the face. The way she held a blade like it was the only truthful thing in her life. The way her voice cracked, just barely, on his name.

That was the moment he broke — and could not show it.

### The Confrontation — "We Were Supposed to Live"

In their final battle, she falters — the brainwashing slips — and she remembers. Not everything. Not clearly. But enough.

Enough to hate what she's become. Enough to reach for the person she once loved.

In a quiet moment before the end:

> **"You were supposed to save me."**
> **"I did."**
> **"Then why didn't you take me with you?"**
> **"…Because they would've only taken you again."**

She realizes then what his life truly cost him. He realizes what hers was stolen for.

For the first time, they are aligned — not by history, but by grief.

### The Sacrifice

Ometrieysa's body becomes unstable — not metaphorically, but magically. Her awakening surges beyond containment: lightning, spirit, raw Dyric energy clawing through the seams of reality.

If she dies uncontrolled, she'll tear open a rift that finishes what the Fracture later becomes.

She begs Maeleus to kill her. He refuses. So she makes the choice for both of them.

She draws the power inward. He holds her — not to stop her, but to anchor her. They become the eye of their own storm.

> **Two people who once wanted a future become the fuse that prevents the end of the world.**

The explosion annihilates high-ranking Church leadership, collapses a ritual that could have shattered Dyradia entirely, and buys [[Ezericht La'Monte]] and the others time to survive.

They die together.

History brands him a traitor. History erases her the moment it finishes using her.

Only a very few know the truth.

## Legacy

The official record says:

- Maeleus betrayed Mehrendt
- He massacred civilians unprovoked
- He was imprisoned for life and died unnamed

The Church says:

- Ometrieysa died in holy service
- She ascended as punishment or reward (interpretations vary)

But the truth — the truth is this:

> **They saved the world, and the world needed them to die as villains so it could sleep at night.**

Maeleus was the knife that cut out the infection. [[Ometrieysa]] was the wound that never healed.

And the world moved on, because it had to.
