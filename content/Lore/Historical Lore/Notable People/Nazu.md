# Nazu

The third of [[Six]]'s four brothers, identical to Six in face and in body, distinct in element. Nazu's element is **wind**. His clothing is green.

## Capabilities

- **Aeromancy.** Nazu manipulates and creates wind. His travelling method of preference is by air, which the chronicle describes him as finding *relaxing*.
- **Scythe.** Nazu carries a scythe as his weapon of choice.
- **Soul-stone.** A soul-stone of matching green colour, carried as an internal anchor for his elemental domain.
- **Pocket-watch teleport.** Like his brothers, Nazu carries a small pocket-watch contraption made by their father which teleports him to the swamp castle on a single press of the central button.

## Temperament

The chronicle records Nazu as *extremely erratic but easier to follow than [[Natas]]* — given to outbursts and to colourful objection but, in most cases, ultimately compliant with the requests of his father. He answered his father's recall to the swamp castle by complaining at length about the pulse of his own bracelet, on the grounds that he was already at the castle.
