# Natas

Eldest of [[Six]]'s four brothers, identical to Six in face and in body, distinct in element. Natas's element is **fire**. His clothing is red.

## Capabilities

- **Pyromancy.** Natas does not merely manipulate fire but creates it. He summoned a fire golem from his lodgings at an unnamed southern town inn and walked out into the street while the inn behind him burned — leaving the staff to deal with both the fire and the unpaid bill simultaneously.
- **Sword.** Natas carries a sword as his weapon of choice, though the chronicle observes that he prefers the element itself.
- **Soul-stone.** A soul-stone of matching red colour, carried as an internal anchor for his elemental domain. The chronicle does not yet record the mechanics of the stone.
- **Pocket-watch teleport.** Like his brothers, Natas carries a small pocket-watch contraption made by their father which teleports him to the swamp castle on a single press of the central button.

## Temperament

The chronicle records Natas as *wild and crazily erratic* — quickest of the four to act on impulse and least concerned with the consequences of his impulses. He answered his father's recall with the enthusiastic prospect of being permitted, by the errand's terms, to kill someone.

## Known Engagements

- **The Burning Inn.** Walked out of an inn whose room he had occupied and whose bill he had not paid, set the building alight by means of a fire-golem he had no intention of recovering, and continued his day's business. See [[Chapter 9 - The Hour the World Turned]].
- **The Town That Was.** Burned the entire southern town to ash in approximately five minutes after receiving from [[Trinity Stolen]] both the gold owed to [[Six]] for the broken mission and a quiet permission to do as he pleased. The fire-golem from the earlier burning inn walked at his shoulder. See [[Chapter 10 - Of Gems and Fire]].
