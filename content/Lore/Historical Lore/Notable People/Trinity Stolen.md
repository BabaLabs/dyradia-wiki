# Trinity Stolen

An agent of the [[Hypostasis Monastery]] of the west borders, deployed into Mehrendt during the Sahlen raids to recruit a company capable of attempting the rescue of the King. Her arrival in the chronicle places her as the first messenger of a much larger political machinery that would later be widely understood to have shaped Dyradia from the shadows of its religious institutions.

## Manner & Capabilities

- **Hooded.** She kept her face covered through her initial encounter with [[Edward Z Zwarf]] and [[Ezericht La'Monte]], by deliberate practice rather than circumstance.
- **Athletic.** Jumped from the top of [[Sahlen]]'s north tower in a single drop and landed without unsettling her robe; entered [[Zant]]'s inn room by jumping to a window-rim no wider than an inch and crossing it without audible sound.
- **Composed.** Spoke evenly and observantly. Her interior commentary, when it surfaced in chronicle, ran wry rather than sentimental.
- **Carried a companion called [[Shift]].** A small creature in her keeping, normally disguised as one of her necklaces. Shift could shift to a form smaller than a fly for the purposes of conveying messages over distance. Trinity used Shift to report to her order's elder — addressed by the respectful title **Gheronda** — within minutes of having identified the Sahlen company as candidates.
- **Presence-tracking.** Could detect and follow the unique *presence* of a known individual at significant distance. Used this to track [[Zant]] through Sahlen at pre-dawn after a brief dream-glimpse of his face.
- **Amplifico.** A spell, channeled through a slim pendant-necklace, that lifted written text off a page and enlarged it in the air for collective reading. Used to make the Veneficus contracts legible to the full company at the contract-signing.

## Order Capabilities

Beyond the [[Veneficus Contracts]] tradition by which she conducted the Sahlen recruitment, Trinity demonstrated through the events of the chronicle several further capabilities of the [[Hypostasis Monastery]]'s practice. The chronicle records the following as canonical:

- **amplifico** — pendant-channeled enlargement of written text into legible air. Used at the Sahlen contract reading.
- **turbato** — confusion-induction over an immediate radius. Used multiple times against pursuing soldiers and inn-clerks.
- **advoco super vestri pactum** *("I summon by your pact")* — invocation that drew chains of fire from earth or floor to bind an incorporeal entity. Used to bind the spectre that rose from [[Alaric La'Monte|Alaric]]'s throne.
- **Absum** — sealing-incantation by which a bound entity was drawn into a faceted white gem. The gem turned red on completion.
- **Brown gem** — parted compacted earth and rubble cleanly into two masses against corridor walls. Used to enter the collapsed chamber tunnel.
- **White gem** — body-preservation in arrayed sets of nine, placed at the feet and shoulders of the corpse. Used on Alaric's body in the chamber to prevent decomposition during conveyance.
- **Translocation papers** — single-use parchments blown on for fixed-destination teleport. Used to travel to [[Dunmisk]].
- **Presence-tracking** — detection and following of the unique presence of a known individual at significant distance.

See [[Chapter 10 - Of Gems and Fire]] for the order's most complete documented exercise of these capabilities.

## Allegiance & Inner Reservation

The chronicle records, of Trinity's relationship to her order, a recurring private commentary at variance with the order's public face. She delivered the Gheronda's *"most sincere apology"* to the dead King at the foot of the throne with a smile she herself recognized, in the same breath, as the smile of *"a woman who had heard, in her own voice, the hypocrisy of the words she had been instructed to deliver."* The chronicle preserves a private *promise* she undertook to carry out on Alaric's behalf — the substance of which she did not, in the chamber, speak aloud loudly enough for the chronicle to record. See [[Chapter 10 - Of Gems and Fire]].

## Non-Human Aspect

When her composure failed her — most documented after an unprovoked arrow-wound from [[Edward Z Zwarf]] outside [[Ezericht La'Monte|Ezericht]]'s shack — Trinity's appearance altered visibly into what the chronicle understands to be her true form: **brown eyes become a glowing pale blue-white; pupils narrow to vertical slits; fine pale markings trace down the cheekbones and up the brow; two small luminous horns rise at the forehead.** She turned the arrow that had wounded her to grey ash in her fingers and knit her leg-wound closed within the span of three breaths.

The tradition, species, or lineage to which Trinity belongs is not yet specified in the chronicle. After the episode at the shack, her ordinary face returned — though the vertical pupils remained. See [[Chapter 5 - Of Cat-Eyes and Contracts]].

## Affiliation

Trinity served Hypostasis Monastery directly. The monastery, though religious in appearance, was, in Trinity's own description, *"long been involved in the ruling of Dyradia, secretly nevertheless."* The full extent of that involvement, and the identity of the King whose rescue she had been sent to broker, are matters the chronicle has not yet recorded.

## First Documented Mission

The recruitment of [[Edward Z Zwarf]], [[Ezericht La'Monte]], [[Six]], and a fourth unnamed companion known to the company only as "the giant" — for a sum of fifteen hundred gold each, plus a five-hundred-gold supplement for additional hands. See [[Chapter 2 - The Monk and the Mission]].
