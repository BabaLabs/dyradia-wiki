# Lauren Fern

A contact of [[Hypostasis Monastery]] stationed at the inn in [[Valham]] in the years surrounding [[The Fracture]]. Recruited to the monastery's network recently — by [[Trinity Stolen]]'s reckoning — after the deaths of her family.

## Manner

Young, alert, and not yet hardened. Operated the front desk of the Valham inn as her cover work. Slept lightly enough to startle to a crossbow when the door opened; not yet practiced enough to *fire* it without thinking, which Trinity gently and unsmilingly corrected her on.

## Known Appearances

- **The Valham Inn.** Received [[Trinity Stolen]] on her flight from [[Sahlen]] after the contract negotiation. See [[Chapter 3 - The Contract and the Camp]].
