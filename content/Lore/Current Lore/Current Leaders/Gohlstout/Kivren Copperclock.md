# Guild-Primary Kivren Copperclock

**Name / Epithets:** Kivren Copperclock, “Primary of the Western Ledger”  
**Role:** Guild-Primary; trade magnate and contract-holder  
**Region:** Gohlstout  
**Seat / Base:** Western bays and merchant houses feeding into Gohl-Khazir  
**Known Allies:** Dock unions; caravan masters; foreign trade partners; artisan consortiums  
**Known Rivals:** Protectionist officials; rival guild primaries; anti-trade populists  
**Public Agenda:** Expand trade capacity and manufactory reach; keep Gohlstout prosperous and independent  
**Triad Lean:** **Pragmatic / variable** (order when it profits; change when it pays)  
**Reputation:** “A smile with a contract behind it.”

### Overview

Kivren Copperclock is the friend of merchants and the headache of regulators. He controls an enormous share of western trade logistics: caravans, shipping schedules, warehousing, and the contracts that keep Gohlstout’s economy humming. People argue over whether he’s a patriot or a profiteer, but nobody argues he’s irrelevant.

### Quick Facts

- Known as the most influential trade voice outside the Deep Council itself
    
- Frequently hosts foreign envoys and negotiates with remarkable ease
    
- Funds civic works, apprenticeships, and reconstruction projects
    
- Has a reputation for paying debts promptly, and collecting them relentlessly
    
- Rarely appears without scribes or contract witnesses
    

### Influence & Methods (Public)

- Uses contracts and mutual obligation to build long-term leverage
    
- Operates trade protection through escorts, insurance, and negotiated access
    
- Supports artisan innovation and infrastructure expansion
    

### Common Rumors (Unverified)

- “He knows a route no map admits.”
    
- “If Copperclock wants something moved quietly, it moves.”
    

### In-World Saying

> “Copperclock doesn’t threaten. He offers terms.”
