# Archivist-Primus Sella Bronzebraid

**Name / Epithets:** Sella Bronzebraid, “Archivist-Primus”  
**Role:** Chief Archivist of the Vaulted Record  
**Region:** Gohlstout  
**Seat / Base:** Gohl-Khazir (Vaulted Record & Ledger Courts)  
**Known Allies:** Judges of the Ledger Courts; scholarly circles; civic stewards  
**Known Rivals:** None openly (often criticized by those who distrust “gatekeepers”)  
**Public Agenda:** Preserve and curate historical record; prevent misuse of sensitive knowledge  
**Triad Lean:** **Convergence-leaning** (shared continuity, controlled narrative)  
**Reputation:** “If it isn’t recorded, it didn’t happen.”

### Overview

Sella Bronzebraid oversees the Vaulted Record: Gohlstout’s most trusted (and most feared) civic institution. In a land that treats memory as a foundation stone, Sella is the keeper of that foundation. She is respected for making history usable, not dramatic; ensuring disputes can be settled, claims can be verified, and cultural continuity remains unbroken.

### Quick Facts

- Considered the final authority on historical verification within Gohlstout
    
- Regularly consulted by judges, guild primaries, and Deep Council envoys
    
- Known for calm speech and precise language
    
- Publicly advocates responsible scholarship and cultural stewardship
    
- Rumored to maintain records going back to pre-Fracture generations
    

### Influence & Methods (Public)

- Controls access to restricted archives through formal petition
    
- Advises the Ledger Courts on precedent and documented claims
    
- Sponsors scribes and archivists in other cities for standardized recordkeeping
    

### Common Rumors (Unverified)

- “She can end a feud with a single page.”
    
- “Some books aren’t lost. They’re _withheld_.”
    

### In-World Saying

> “Bronzebraid never argues; she cites.”
