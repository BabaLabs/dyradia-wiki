# The Resonantwright Circle

**Name / Epithets:** The Resonantwright Circle, “The Frequency Artisans”  
**Role:** Collegiate guild-circle of specialized artificers and scholars  
**Region:** Gohlstout  
**Seat / Base:** Research halls, guild workshops, and civic colleges in Gohl-Khazir  
**Known Allies:** Civic planners; engineers; select guild primaries; academic patrons  
**Known Rivals:** Old-guard traditionalists; superstition-minded factions; anti-scholar populists  
**Public Agenda:** Study post-Fracture phenomena; improve stability and safety infrastructure through reliable methods  
**Triad Lean:** **Stasis / Convergence-adjacent** (order through method and alignment)  
**Reputation:** “They don’t cast; they calibrate.”

### Overview

The Resonantwright Circle is Gohlstout’s most famous “new school” institution: artificers and scholars who approach the world as measurable systems. Their work is credited with improvements to civic safety, engineering, and certain forms of post-Fracture mitigation. Outsiders call them mages. They politely correct outsiders.

### Quick Facts

- Known for strict methodology and controlled experimentation
    
- Publicly presents research as civil service, not power
    
- Hosts apprenticeships coveted across multiple Holds
    
- Frequently consulted during reconstruction and infrastructure planning
    
- Often blamed (fairly or not) for “meddling with things best left alone”
    

### Influence & Methods (Public)

- Publishes limited research summaries for civic use
    
- Builds prototypes for safety infrastructure and hazard warning systems
    
- Advises officials on risk mitigation in uncertain environments
    

### Common Rumors (Unverified)

- “They can make a bridge ignore wind.”
    
- “They’ve got a room where the world sounds different.”
    

### In-World Saying

> “Ask a Resonantwright for a miracle and you’ll get a measurement.”
