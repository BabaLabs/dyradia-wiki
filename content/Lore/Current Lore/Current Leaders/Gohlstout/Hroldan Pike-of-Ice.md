# Warden Hroldan Pike-of-Ice

**Name / Epithets:** Hroldan Pike-of-Ice, “Warden of the Deep Frozen”  
**Role:** Frontier Warden and Pass-Master of the northern watch  
**Region:** Gohlstout  
**Seat / Base:** Gohl-Fhronim (watchposts, pass roads, winter stations)  
**Known Allies:** Route-wardens; mountain guides; frontier patrol captains  
**Known Rivals:** Smugglers; reckless expeditions; those who ignore posted closures  
**Public Agenda:** Keep the mountain passes safe; limit loss of life; enforce closures without apology  
**Triad Lean:** **Stasis-leaning** (safety over risk)  
**Reputation:** “The mountain doesn’t negotiate.”

### Overview

Hroldan Pike-of-Ice is the name travelers learn when they plan to cross Gohlstout’s harshest highlands. He oversees the watchposts that control entry into the most dangerous northern terrain: issuing closures, escort contracts, and emergency response when storms turn routes into graves. He is not popular. He is trusted.

### Quick Facts

- Commands multiple pass stations and rotating winter patrols
    
- Known for refusing bribes and refusing heroics in equal measure
    
- Maintains blacklists for routes deemed too hazardous
    
- Tolerates outsiders who follow protocol; ejects those who don’t
    
- Has personally led rescue efforts that became local legend
    

### Influence & Methods (Public)

- Enforces travel permits and seasonal closures
    
- Coordinates rapid-response rescues when weather allows
    
- Maintains official route guides updated by patrol reports
    

### Common Rumors (Unverified)

- “He’s turned back expeditions that had noble signatures.”
    
- “He’s seen things in the storm he refuses to name.”
    

### In-World Saying

> “If Pike-of-Ice says _no_, the smart answer is _yes, sir_.”
