# High Seat Durnek “Stoneweight” Varr

**Name / Epithets:** Durnek Varr, “Stoneweight”  
**Role:** High Seat of the Deep Council  
**Region:** Gohlstout  
**Seat / Base:** Gohl-Khazir (The Deep Hold)  
**Known Allies:** The Deep Council; major guild primaries  
**Known Rivals:** None openly; tense relations with foreign envoys pushing treaty compliance  
**Public Agenda:** Preserve dwarven stability and sovereignty while keeping trade viable  
**Triad Lean:** **Stasis-leaning**  
**Reputation:** “Slow voice, heavy decision.”

### Overview

Durnek Varr is the presiding voice of Gohlstout’s Deep Council: less a king than a load-bearing keystone. He is widely credited with keeping the Holds stable through a world that still hasn’t fully recovered from the Fracture era. To outsiders he seems cautious; to dwarves he seems _responsible._

### Quick Facts

- Known for measured decisions and a calm public presence
    
- Prioritizes stability, trade continuity, and dwarven autonomy
    
- Prefers negotiation over force, but does not bluff when he sets a boundary
    
- Treats law and infrastructure as inseparable parts of governance
    
- Rarely comments on foreign politics, but chooses his words carefully when he does
    

### Influence & Methods (Public)

- Uses councils, contracts, and guild leverage rather than decrees
    
- Sends envoys and inspectors more often than soldiers
    
- Stabilizes problems quietly and expects the same from others
    

### Common Rumors (Unverified)

- “He has a private list of things the Holds will never risk again.”
    
- “If he ever raises his voice, it means the decision was made days ago.”
    

### In-World Saying

> “Stoneweight doesn’t threaten. Stoneweight measures.”
