# High Banner Varka “Red-Sand” Umasiel

**Name / Epithets:** Varka Umasiel, “Red-Sand”  
**Role:** High Banner of Avalon  
**Region:** Avalon  
**Seat / Base:** The Banner Moot; foothill settlements beneath the Fractured Spires  
**Known Allies:** Warbands favoring unity; riverfolk leaders; frontier defenders  
**Known Rivals:** Foreign checkpoints; treaty enforcers; would-be centralizers  
**Public Agenda:** Keep Avalon independent and cohesive as outside pressures rise  
**Triad Lean:** **Resistance-leaning** (anti-Stasis by nature)  
**Reputation:** “A blade with a spine.”

### Overview

Varka “Red-Sand” Umasiel is the current High Banner of Avalon, recognized by enough warbands to hold the Moot together. Her leadership is less about ruling and more about keeping Avalon from tearing itself apart under pressure. She is seen as a practical protector: tough, direct, and unromantic about what survival costs.

### Quick Facts

- Widely respected as a unifying commander
    
- Treats “independence” as a living thing that must be defended daily
    
- Values oaths and loyalty, but distrusts institutions that outlive their purpose
    
- Keeps close watch on the river border and treaty disputes
    
- Known to reward competence, even from outsiders, if they respect Avalon’s lines
    

### Influence & Methods (Public)

- Maintains unity through shared defense and negotiated oaths
    
- Uses mobile forces and flexible borders rather than fortified control
    
- Prefers deterrence and reputation over open war
    

### Common Rumors (Unverified)

- “She’s trying to stop a war she knows is coming.”
    
- “If the Moot ever fractures, she’ll be the last one holding the line.”
    

### In-World Saying

> “Red-Sand doesn’t build walls. She makes the ground expensive to take.”
