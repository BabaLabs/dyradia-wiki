# River-Marshal Thane Oric Sablewake

**Name / Epithets:** Thane Oric Sablewake, “River-Marshal”  
**Role:** Commander of river patrols and border response  
**Region:** Avalon  
**Seat / Base:** River corridor posts, estuary towns, and patrol routes  
**Known Allies:** Riverfolk captains; border militias; settlement speakers along the waterline  
**Known Rivals:** Foreign checkpoints; overreaching treaty forces; river profiteers  
**Public Agenda:** Protect river access and travelers; prevent occupation-by-policy  
**Triad Lean:** **Resistance-leaning** (anti-Stasis enforcement)  
**Reputation:** “Fair warning, hard consequence.”

### Overview

Oric Sablewake is Avalon’s visible edge on the water: tasked with keeping the river corridor open and preventing it from becoming a foreign choke chain. He is respected as disciplined and despised as inflexible, depending on who you ask. To those who live by the river, he is a necessary guardian.

### Quick Facts

- Leads patrols that respond fast to river incidents
    
- Known for strict conduct and clear lines of authority
    
- Maintains agreements with many river communities
    
- Treats “paper authority” with suspicion
    
- Prefers deterrence over war, but prepares like war is inevitable
    

### Influence & Methods (Public)

- Controls patrol timing and checkpoint pressure from Avalon’s side
    
- Negotiates “river safety covenants” with settlement speakers
    
- Coordinates escorts for vulnerable caravans and boats
    

### Common Rumors (Unverified)

- “He memorizes every crossing point.”
    
- “He’s stopped incidents you’ll never hear about.”
    

### In-World Saying

> “Sablewake rarely picks fights; he prevents them from spreading.”
