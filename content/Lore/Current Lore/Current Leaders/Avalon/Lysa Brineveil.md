# Hearth-Seer Lysa Brineveil

**Name / Epithets:** Lysa Brineveil, “Hearth-Seer”  
**Role:** Cultural guide, omen-reader, and community anchor  
**Region:** Avalon  
**Seat / Base:** Traveling hearth circles; border settlements; lake-route communities  
**Known Allies:** Elders, healers, camp-speakers, and settled families  
**Known Rivals:** Fearmongers; exploitative warlords; anyone who turns hardship into cruelty  
**Public Agenda:** Keep communities cohesive; reduce panic; protect traditions and morale  
**Triad Lean:** **Convergence-leaning** (cohesion and calm)  
**Reputation:** “She hears storms before they arrive.”

### Overview

Lysa Brineveil is not a war leader, and does not claim authority, yet her words can settle a camp faster than a command. She travels between settlements, tending to the social fabric that keeps Avalon from turning on itself. Many see her as spiritual; many see her as practical; most simply see her as needed.

### Quick Facts

- Known for preventing feuds and calming warband disputes
    
- Often present at births, funerals, and reconciliation rites
    
- Trusted by communities that distrust formal leadership
    
- Maintains old oral histories and community sayings
    
- Treats outsiders carefully but not unkindly
    

### Influence & Methods (Public)

- Uses hearth rites, shared meals, and deliberate storytelling to unify groups
    
- Offers counsel to speakers and elders rather than issuing directives
    
- Helps settlements “name their fears” so they don’t become violence
    

### Common Rumors (Unverified)

- “She’s never wrong about rain.”
    
- “Sometimes she speaks like she already knows what you’ll choose.”
    

### In-World Saying

> “When Brineveil sits by your fire, your anger remembers that it's tired.”
