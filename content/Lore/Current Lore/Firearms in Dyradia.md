# Firearms in Dyradia

## Tech Level

Dyradian firearms are comparable to like Ivalice from FF12. They have guns, and even things resembling cannons. But it's not full-blown Magitech.

---

## What That Means in Practice

Firearms exist in Dyradia, but they are:

- Specialized, not mass-produced
- Expensive
- Temperamental
- Slow to master
- Situational

Melee and bows still dominate the battlefield. Guns are *tools*, not tech supremacy. Most NPCs have seen guns, but don't own them. Firearms are respected, not feared.

This answers the classic question — "Why doesn't everyone just use guns?" — with: because they're expensive, temperamental, slow to master, and situational. Very Ivalice. Very Dyradia.

---

## Relationship to Magic and Decline

Firearms fit the present era for layered reasons:

- **Post-Awakening decline** — old doctrines linger; firearm mastery is one of the surviving specialized arts
- **Diminishing magic** — see [[State of Magic in Dyradia]]; as magic becomes less reliable, mundane ranged weapons quietly gain relevance
- **Interstitial instability** — ranged discipline matters more in a world where space and magic both behave unpredictably
- **Church legacy** — religious cultures inherited from the Church of Prim tend to suspect anything that isn't traditional, including firearms

A gunslinger in Dyradia is a walking contradiction — a reminder of old progress that never fully vanished, someone the world hasn't decided how to categorize yet.

---

## Artifice (Cannon-craft and Engineered Force)

Where firearms exist on a continuum with magical engineering, the more elaborate end shades into artifice:

- *Anchored spell matrices*
- *Contained resonance emitters*
- *Portable siege formulae*

A practitioner working in this space might describe their craft as:

> "I don't trust raw magic anymore — so I give it a casing."

Artifice in Dyradia is more academic and ritualized than experimental. Its practitioners refine lost principles from the [[The Academic Awakening]]; they are not inventing technology generations ahead of everyone else. They compensate. They stabilize. They approximate what used to be possible.

---

## Cultural Reception

- **Mehrendt:** uses firearms for specialized military and law-enforcement units; production is regulated
- **Fey'thra:** views them as a human curiosity, neither embraced nor banned
- **Gohlstout:** appreciates the craftsmanship; preserves designs and gunsmith traditions
- **Avalon:** values firearms for their independence from formal infrastructure
