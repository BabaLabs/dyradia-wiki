# The Dyradian Calendar

*Standard Civil Calendar (12 months)*

Most nations use a shared civic calendar for trade, law, and scholarship. Regional names exist (especially in [[Fey'thra]] and [[Gohlstout]]), but the following month names are widely understood in Common across Dyradia.

For year-numbering and the Fracture Reckoning (BF/AF) system, see [[Major Historical Eras]].

---

## Seasons

- **Vernal** (Spring): months 1–3
- **Highsun** (Summer): months 4–6
- **Harvest** (Autumn): months 7–9
- **Deepfrost** (Winter): months 10–12

*(These are "civil" seasons used for records; local climates still vary.)*

---

## The 12 Months

### 1) **Dawnswell** *(DS)*

**Vibe:** the year "opens," trade resumes, roads become passable
**Common note:** contracts, travel, rebuilding plans

### 2) **Greenrise** *(GR)*

**Vibe:** growth, marsh bloom, first floods
**Common note:** planting, river rights, Fey'thran rites increase

### 3) **Thawtide** *(TT)*

**Vibe:** meltwater, unstable roads, hungry currents
**Common note:** Gohl-Fhronim runoff; bridge inspections

### 4) **Brightwake** *(BW)*

**Vibe:** clear skies, long days, safe sailing begins
**Common note:** peak coastal travel, festivals, major caravans

### 5) **Goldmere** *(GM)*

**Vibe:** wealth-month; trade peak, tariffs, guild disputes
**Common note:** most weddings; most political maneuvering

### 6) **Hearthflame** *(HF)*

**Vibe:** heat, dust, tempers; warband season in Avalon
**Common note:** tournaments, militia drills, border flare-ups

### 7) **Sicklefall** *(SF)*

**Vibe:** harvest begins, storms return in bursts
**Common note:** grain tallies, port inspections, tax season

### 8) **Redleaf** *(RL)*

**Vibe:** travel turns dangerous; fog, rot, and early chills
**Common note:** illness season; Fey paths "get weird"

### 9) **Emberturn** *(ET)*

**Vibe:** the last warm breath of the year
**Common note:** final shipping pushes; closing trade contracts

### 10) **Gloamreach** *(GL)*

**Vibe:** dark comes early; people stay indoors; secrets thrive
**Common note:** courts, trials, political purges, quiet violence

### 11) **Deepfrost** *(DF)*

**Vibe:** true winter; mountain passes close; sea turns cruel
**Common note:** Gohlstout closures; storm losses on the Expanse

### 12) **Stillnight** *(SN)*

**Vibe:** year-end hush; remembrance and omen-reading
**Common note:** memorial rites; vows; "thin nights"

---

## Common Date Format

**Scholarly:** *14 Goldmere, 327 AF*
**Shorthand:** *14 GM 327 AF*

---

## Folk Sayings

- *"Don't promise in Stillnight."* (oaths cling)
- *"Thawtide lies."* (roads and rivers betray)
- *"Goldmere buys smiles."* (politics + commerce month)
- *"Gloamreach remembers what you did."* (court season)

---

## Regional Flavor Variants

- **[[Fey'thra]]** often calls Stillnight **"Veilmonth"** (thin places, long shadows)
- **[[Gohlstout]]** often calls Thawtide **"Runoff"** (practical dwarven naming)
- **[[Avalon]]** calls Hearthflame **"Warheat"** (training, raids, tempers)
