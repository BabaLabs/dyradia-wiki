# Maerovyn, the Bell That Binds

A Regent of [[The Liminal Court]].

## Also Called

- The Oath-Bell
- The Sound After Promise
- The Binder of Breath
- The Witness in the Word

## Domain

Maerovyn governs oaths, vows, bargains, contracts, promises, and the moment spoken words become binding.

Their threshold is the instant before and after commitment.

> *Before the words, one is free.*
> *After the words, one is changed.*

## Symbols

- Bells
- Knotted cords
- Wax seals
- Contracts
- Rings
- Bound hands
- A bell heard after a promise is spoken

## Temperament

Maerovyn is stern, formal, and dangerously fair. They do not punish broken promises because promises are sentimental. They punish broken promises because vows are structures, and structures collapse when their bindings fail.

They are invoked in marriages, alliances, treaties, contracts, debts, and magical bargains.

## Common Saying

> *A vow is a bridge built from breath. Do not cross it lightly.*
