# Lysanthir, the Candle Between

A Regent of [[The Liminal Court]].

## Also Called

- The Small Light
- The Lantern in the Wrong Place
- The Guide Through Unkind Dark
- The Flame That Waits

## Domain

Lysanthir governs guidance, safe passage in darkness, wayfinding, hope during transition, and the fragile light that helps travelers cross from danger into shelter.

Their thresholds often appear during uncertainty rather than death: lost roads, dark woods, storm crossings, underground routes, and moments when despair threatens to become surrender.

## Symbols

- Candles
- Lanterns
- Fireflies
- Hearthlight seen from far away
- A flame that does not go out in rain
- Warm light beneath a closed door

## Temperament

Lysanthir is among the gentler Regents, though still deeply fey. Their aid often comes with lessons about trust, patience, and the danger of mistaking any light for the right light.

They are invoked by travelers, children, refugees, healers, and those searching for someone lost.

## Common Saying

> *Not every light leads home. But the right one waits.*
