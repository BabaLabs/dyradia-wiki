# Thorne

**Character:** Fieldmaster Rennel Thorne

Thorne is **middle-aged** (late 30s to mid 40s), visibly seasoned, but still in peak fighting shape. He has a **Alan Ritchson-type build**: **broad and burly**, thick shoulders and chest, powerful arms and back. Yet he moves like he’s **dangerously fast** for his size.

**Face & hair:**

- Face reads “seen some shit,” but not gloomy. More calm, controlled competence.
- Strong jaw, usually with short stubble (5 o’clock shadow or trimmed rough beard).
- Nose slightly crooked or previously broken.
- Eyes steady and observant (suggest hazel or gray-green).
- Hair is **mid-length, almost to shoulders**, slightly wavy, often loosely tied back; dark brown with subtle early silver at the temples.

**Scars:**

- Multiple practical scars across arms/neck/forearms; not stylized, just lived-in.
- One subtle scar across an eyebrow/temple.
- Signature scar: a **magical brand** on the **upper left collarbone/upper chest** (visible if cloak shifts). The brand is a **perfect circular halo** (about silver-dollar size) with a **clean surgical break** at the lower-right edge. Inside the halo is a centered **vertical spine line** with **three small cross-marks** along it (top/mid/bottom). Scar tissue is unnaturally smooth and slightly raised, like wax set into skin. It's holy and precise, not messy.

**Clothing & silhouette:**  
Thorne always wears a **heavy hooded cloak** with a **long cape-like fall**. This cloak is iconic: he’s recognizable by it.

- Colors: charcoal/deep green-black outer fabric; inner lining muted moss green.
- Practical layered clothing underneath: fitted travel tunic, reinforced leather/armor pieces built for mobility.
- Boots are rugged and made for marsh/road travel.
- He carries small utilitarian items: a chalk case or ward-chalk tube, a few plain talisman cords that look like tools rather than jewelry.

**Weapon:**

- Carries a **small plain dagger** at the belt (unadorned, worn leather wrap). It looks like a utility blade, not a showpiece. He almost never draws it.
- No flashy weapons ever. He looks dangerous because of posture and presence.

**Posture & vibe:**

- Stands slightly angled toward exits without looking paranoid.
- Quiet, controlled, mentor energy.
- He should feel like a protector who can flip into terrifying efficiency instantly.
- Expression: calm half-smile OR neutral watchfulness; no theatrical grimacing.
