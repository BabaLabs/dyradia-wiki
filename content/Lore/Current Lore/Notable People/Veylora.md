# Veylora, the Mirror Warden

A Regent of [[The Liminal Court]].

## Also Called

- She Who Watches Back
- The Keeper Behind Glass
- The True Face
- The Mask-Breaker

## Domain

Veylora governs mirrors, reflections, selfhood, recognition, identity, masks, and the dangerous moment when someone sees what they truly are.

Her thresholds are inward-facing. They concern the passage between:

- lie and truth,
- mask and face,
- name and self,
- memory and identity,
- what one performs and what one is.

## Symbols

- Mirrors
- Still water
- Silver glass
- Masks split down the center
- Eyes reflected where none should be
- A face appearing half a breath late in a mirror

## Temperament

Veylora is neither cruel nor kind. She is exacting. Her gifts are clarity, and clarity is not always mercy.

She is invoked in rites of confession, identity, transformation, and self-recognition. She is feared by spies, shapeshifters, oath-breakers, and those who survive by forgetting themselves.

## Common Saying

> *Do not ask the mirror who you are unless you are prepared to lose who you were.*
