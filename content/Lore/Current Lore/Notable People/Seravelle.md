# Seravelle, the Thorn-Smile Host

A Regent of [[The Liminal Court]].

## Also Called

- The Guest-Law Queen
- The Host with Hidden Teeth
- The Banquet at the Threshold
- The Welcome That Watches

## Domain

Seravelle governs hospitality, sanctuary, invitation, guest-right, host-law, and the danger hidden inside being welcomed.

Her threshold is the moment a stranger becomes a guest, or a guest becomes a prisoner.

## Symbols

- Open doors
- Tables set for one too many
- Thorned vines
- Smiling masks
- Red fruit
- Salt shared across a table
- A chair pulled out before anyone asks

## Temperament

Seravelle is charming, gracious, and terrifying. She is generous to those who honor guest-law and merciless toward those who abuse sanctuary.

She is invoked before feasts, negotiations, diplomatic visits, and sheltering strangers.

## Common Saying

> *A welcome is a door with teeth. Mind whose table you sit at.*
