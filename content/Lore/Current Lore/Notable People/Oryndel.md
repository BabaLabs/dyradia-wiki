# Oryndel, the River of Steps

A Regent of [[The Liminal Court]].

## Also Called

- The Road That Remembers
- The Procession Without End
- The One Step More
- The Arriving Current

## Domain

Oryndel governs roads, routes, journeys, timing, processions, pilgrimages, and the strange inevitability of arriving where one was always going.

Their domain includes both physical and narrative movement: the road walked, the path chosen, the route avoided, and the journey one only understands after reaching its end.

## Symbols

- Rivers
- Footprints
- Road dust
- Walking sticks
- Milestones
- Shoes left at crossroads
- A path that seems longer or shorter than it should

## Temperament

Oryndel is patient and relentless. They rarely force movement, but they make stillness difficult when the time for departure has come.

They are invoked by pilgrims, couriers, caravans, exiles, refugees, and those who fear or crave change.

## Common Saying

> *The road does not chase. It waits until you are ready to admit you are already on it.*
