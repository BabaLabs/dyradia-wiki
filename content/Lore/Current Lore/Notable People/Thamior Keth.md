# Thamior Keth, the Key-Bearer

A Regent of [[The Liminal Court]].

## Also Called

- The Keeper of Locks
- The Permissioned Hand
- The One Who Opens Only Once
- The Teeth of the Door

## Domain

Thamior Keth governs doors, locks, keys, access, permission, and the difference between entering and being allowed in.

He is not merely concerned with physical doors. His laws apply to guarded knowledge, sealed names, hidden rooms, protected memories, and places that open only under the correct terms.

## Symbols

- Keys
- Keyholes
- Locked doors
- Iron rings
- Unmarked gates
- A door that appears where no door should be
- A key found in one's pocket with no memory of receiving it

## Temperament

Thamior Keth is precise, transactional, and unforgiving of trespass. He does not hate thieves, but he respects only those who understand the difference between theft, invitation, and permission.

He is invoked by jailers, wardens, locksmiths, archivists, smugglers, and those who seek forbidden rooms.

## Common Saying

> *A door opened without permission still remembers the hand.*
