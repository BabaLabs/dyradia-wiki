Sites within Mehrendt. The capital, the institutions, the prison nobody comes back from.

- **[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Mehrendt/Valemarch|Valemarch]]** *(CrownSpire)* — the capital. Courts, ministries, and the engine room of the realm.
- **[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Mehrendt/Crownwater Penitentiary|Crownwater Penitentiary]]** *(the Dreadworks)* — a fortress-prison cut into a sea cliff. No returns.
- **[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Mehrendt/Luminmere Collegium|Luminmere Collegium]]** — the mage's college, and magic's acceptable public face.
- **[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Mehrendt/Greenward Annex|Greenward Annex]]** — its border campus, closer to the living edge of things.
- **[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Mehrendt/Fort Glasswake|Fort Glasswake]]** — officially a training ground. Actually watching the Church island.
- **[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Mehrendt/Northwatch Haven|Northwatch Haven]]** — the northern port, and the fast route to Gohlstout.
- **[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Mehrendt/Stagspire|Stagspire]]** — a hunters' guildhold big enough to behave like a town.
- **[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Mehrendt/Saltveil Commune|Saltveil Commune]]** — a fishing colony that stays out of trouble by being indispensable.
- **[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Mehrendt/Waymarch|Waymarch]]** — a waystop town. Too small to matter, too useful to ignore.
