## 8) Secondary campus near Fey’thra, east of Dawnmere

### **Greenward Annex**

_(alt names: **Feyline Campus**)_

**Nation:** Mehrendt (border-adjacent)  
**Type:** Satellite Campus / Field Institute  
**Known For:** stronger talent, practical resonance studies, Fey’thran collaboration rumors  
**Triad Weather:** Convergence-leaning (students/staff attracted to synthesis)

### Overview

Greenward Annex is smaller, sharper, and closer to the living edge of magic. Being near Fey’thra changes what students see as “normal.” The Annex produces some of the finest minds of the Collegium system; and a steady stream of political headaches.

### Governance & Faces

- **Dean Mirel Voss** - brilliant, stubborn, protective of her students
    
- **Fieldmaster Rennel Thorne** - runs expeditions and keeps casualties low
    

### Rumors

- **True:** The Annex has informal ties to Fey’thran scholars.
    
- **Half:** Some students practice “unapproved” methods out of necessity.
    
- **False:** The Annex is a cult.
    

### Minor Plot Pressure

- Border tensions spike; the Annex becomes a liability.
    
- A Fey’thran grove requests help with an anomaly...quietly.
    
- A student’s thesis attracts the wrong kind of attention.
