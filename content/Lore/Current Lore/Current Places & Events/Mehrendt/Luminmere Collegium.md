## 7) Mage’s College south of Crownspire, lakeside

### Lake name: **Lake Luminmere**
### College: **Luminmere Collegium**

_(alt names: **Spirelake Academy**)_

**Nation:** Mehrendt  
**Type:** Arcane Institute / Public Academy  
**Known For:** practical magic education, civic spellcraft, research ethics debates  
**Triad Weather:** contested (Stasis pressure outside; Renewal curiosity within)

### Overview

Luminmere Collegium is Mehrendt’s “acceptable face” of magic: sanctioned study, controlled practice, and a public argument that knowledge can be safe. It’s open to outsiders under rules… which makes it both respected and constantly targeted by those who fear what it represents.

### Governance & Faces

- **Provost Selwyn Arct** - careful reformer, speaks in measured promises
    
- **Warden-Registrar Hessa Quill** - enforces rules, tracks incidents, keeps the peace
    

### Rumors

- **True:** The Collegium has protocols for “anomalous resonance events.”
    
- **Half:** Avalon recruiters frequent its outer circles.
    
- **False:** It trains Awoken.
    

### Minor Plot Pressure

- Someone is sabotaging public demonstrations to discredit the school.
    
- A student vanishes into the lake at night, then returns… wrong-footed.
    
- A Crownspire inspector arrives with new compliance demands.
