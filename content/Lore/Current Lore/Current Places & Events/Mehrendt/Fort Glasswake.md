## 4) Western/Southern Military & Intelligence Outpost

### **Fort Glasswake**

_(alt names: **Westmarch Bastion**)_

**Nation:** Mehrendt  
**Type:** Fortified Training Grounds / Intelligence Station  
**Known For:** drills, war games, signals watch, quiet operations  
**Triad Weather:** Stasis-heavy

### Overview

Officially, Fort Glasswake is a training installation: disciplined troops, clean formations, and endless drills. Unofficially, it watches the sea and the southern routes—especially anything coming to and from the Church island.

### Governance & Faces

- **Commandant Veyrin Lask** - professional, cold, obsessed with readiness
    
- **“Quartermaster” Sera Dain** - supplies officer who asks too-smart questions
    

### Rumors

- **True:** Signals interception and courier capture happen regularly.
    
- **Half:** There are “unmarked boats” that leave at night.
    
- **False:** The fort is a prison.
    

### Minor Plot Pressure

- A training exercise “accidentally” kills someone who wasn’t supposed to be there.
    
- A spy is caught, and the fort needs outsiders to transport them quietly.
    
- Strange church relics start showing up in fort stores.
