# Valemarch — _Crownspire_

**Type:** Capital City  
**Nation:** Mehrendt  
**Region:** The peninsula’s civic heartland (inland enough to be defensible; close enough to command trade)  
**Known For:** Crown Courts, royal ministries, treaty chambers, “the engine room of Mehrendt”  
**Strategic Value:** Seat of the Crown + legal authority + administrative control over accords  
**Triad Weather:** Stasis influence is strongest here (law, compliance, institutions)

### Overview

Valemarch—called **Crownspire** in formal Common—is the capital of Mehrendt and the place where the kingdom becomes more than borders on a map. It is a city built around governance: courts that set precedent for the whole realm, ministries that track everything from tariffs to census ledgers, and chambers where treaties are drafted, revised, and enforced. Where other cities _live_, Valemarch _directs_.

### Historical Note: The Capital Before the Capital

Valemarch is **not** Mehrendt's first capital. The pre-Fracture capital was [[Nohlle]] — a coastal city of central plaza, wide canal, and seaward orientation that served as the seat of the legitimate royal court through the late Church Ascendancy and into the Fracture year itself. Nohlle was the site of the [[The Cardinal|Cardinal/Gheronda]]'s public address transforming the regicide of King [[Alaric La'Monte]] into the founding propaganda of the Church's anti-magic war, and was the site of [[Edward Z Zwarf|Z]]'s first uncontrolled Awakening in the years that followed — a fire-Awakening that destroyed a district of the city outright. Successive waves of the war damaged the rest of the city across the early Fracture decades; by the time of the Severing (41 AF), the city was largely abandoned.

**Valemarch was rebuilt** as Mehrendt's new capital in the early Stabilization era (~50-100 AF) as a deliberate civic break with the pre-Fracture order. Where Nohlle had been coastal, ceremonial, and aligned with the Church's high doctrine, Valemarch was inland, bureaucratic, and aligned with the legal-procedural ethos that the Stabilization era recognized as the safer mode of governance. The naming as *Crownspire* in formal Common explicitly invoked the new Crown as distinct from the old. See [[Nohlle]] for the pre-Fracture context.

### Quick Facts

- Official seat of Mehrendt’s Crown and Crown Courts
    
- Known for layered bureaucracy and ritualized civic procedure
    
- A magnet for envoys, petitioners, litigants, and ambitious guild representatives
    
- Public order is strong; enforcement tends to be quiet and efficient
    
- Citizens are proud of competence—even when they complain about it
    

### City Character (Public)

- **Crown District:** courts, ministries, treaty halls, formal diplomacy
    
- **Spireward:** old noble houses, legacy guild estates, high civic offices
    
- **Ledgerways:** registries, archives, customs offices, professional scribes
    
- **Ring Wards:** dense residential belts supporting the city’s vast civil machine
    
- **Gateworks:** infrastructure hubs, garrisons, and transit chokepoints
    

### What People Say (In-World)

> “Valemarch doesn’t shout. It _decides_.”
