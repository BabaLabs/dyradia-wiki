### Overview

The northern sea is the artery that keeps Mehrendt’s peninsula connected to the wider world. Its trade routes are vital, but its temperament is infamous: weather shifts fast, and certain currents make some sea journeys safer than long overland caravans. Mariners whisper about islands that appear on no official map—not because they’re magical, but because no one agrees who controls them.

### Quick Facts

- Primary sea access for Mehrendt’s coastlines
    
- Supports a web of merchant lanes and privateer rumors
    
- Storm seasons are well known; captains plan around them
    
- Archipelagos exist off several coasts (ownership often disputed)
    
- Travel by sea is sometimes faster than crossing the peninsula by land
