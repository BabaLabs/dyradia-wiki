### Overview

The river-estuary corridor is where Dyradia’s politics become tangible. To Mehrendt, it is a trade artery and a lifeline. To Avalon, it is shared blood—no crown gets to claim it. To Fey’thra, it is water that must remain alive and unpoisoned by ideology. Those competing truths make the corridor an ever-simmering border zone where law, patrols, and local communities collide.

### Quick Facts

- Major trade route linking inland and coastal economies
    
- Known for border checkpoints, inspections, and periodic skirmishes
    
- Riverfolk communities often have mixed loyalties and mixed heritage
    
- Control shifts by season, treaties, and practical reality (floods, drought, patrol capacity)
    
- A frequent “flashpoint region” where political philosophy becomes policy enforcement
