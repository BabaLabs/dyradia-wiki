# Beyond the Interstice

### 🌫️ Far East / South-East Region

**Beyond the Interstice** _(common term)_

- No agreed maps
    
- Sky described as “heavy,” “wrong,” or “pressed downward”
    
- Scholars argue whether this land is _ahead of_ or _behind_ the rest of Dyradia in time
