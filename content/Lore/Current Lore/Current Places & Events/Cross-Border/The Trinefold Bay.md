### Overview

This massive bay is one of Dyradia’s most politically important bodies of water because it does something land rarely permits: it makes borders porous. Ships, fisheries, and port agreements create unavoidable interdependence between Mehrendt, Fey’thra, and Gohlstout. When relations are warm, it’s a thriving trade basin. When relations sour, it becomes a silent chessboard.

### Quick Facts

- Supports coastal trade and shared fisheries
    
- Home to multiple port towns with mixed cultural presence
    
- Diplomacy here tends to be practical: tariffs, access, and safety corridors
    
- Smuggling accusations are common (proof is rare)
    
- Storm and fog patterns can isolate communities quickly
