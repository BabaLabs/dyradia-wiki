# GOHLSTOUT (Modern Era)

### “The World Beneath the World”

**Capital:** **Gohl-Khazir** (_The Deep Hold_)  
**Mountain Ranges:**

- **Gohl-Fhronim** (_The Deep Frozen_, northern glacial heights)
    
- **The Temperate Crowns** (eastern ranges sheltering Gohl-Khazir)
    

Gohlstout is not a kingdom in the way others are.  
It is a **system**: engineered, layered, redundant, and patient.

---

## 1) Government

**Form:** Stratified Clanship Confederation  
**Ruling Body:** **The Stonebinding Conclave**

There is **no king**.

Instead, power is distributed across three interlocking authorities:

### 🜨 The High Clans (Material Authority)

- Control mines, forges, labor, and logistics
    
- Clan seats are hereditary, but leadership within a clan is earned
    
- Wealth alone does not grant authority... _competence does_
    

### 🜂 The Deepwrights (Infrastructure Authority)

- Engineers, architects, and tunnel-masters
    
- Control transport networks, ventilation, water flow, and structural safety
    
- Can overrule clans on matters of “existential integrity”
    

### 🜃 The Runeholders (Continuity Authority)

- Lorekeepers, historians, runesmiths
    
- Custodians of pre-Fracture knowledge
    
- Decide what techniques are **too dangerous to relearn**
    

All three must agree for world-scale decisions.  
Nothing moves quickly, but nothing collapses easily.

---

## 2) Power Centers

### 🏔️ Gohl-Khazir: The Deep Hold

A colossal subterranean city carved into stable eastern mountains.

Key features:

- **The Great Helix:** a spiraling vertical transit shaft connecting layers
    
- **The Founding Vaults:** oldest surviving halls, sealed during the Fracture
    
- **The Breathways:** vast ventilation arteries feeding the city
    
- **The Deepwater Locks:** control subterranean freshwater inflows
    

Gohl-Khazir is proof that **the Fracture did not break everything** — just the things built too quickly.

---

### 🔨 The Deepwright Orders

Independent from clans, fiercely neutral.

They:

- Inspect every major structure
    
- Shut down unsafe operations without negotiation
    
- Decide when surface construction is _too unstable_
    

They are feared — but never challenged.

---

### 📜 The Runeholds

Archives carved into living stone.

- Contain forbidden schematics
    
- Pre-Fracture runic systems
    
- Records of Interstice experiments that _almost_ worked
    

Many Runeholders believe:

> “The Fracture was not caused by ambition — but by impatience.”

---

## 3) Magic Climate

**Public stance:** “Magic is a tool. Tools break.”  
**Private truth:** “Magic is becoming unreliable again.”

Dwarven magic is:

- Runic
    
- Structural
    
- Redundant
    
- Slow to cast, slow to fail
    

Post-Fracture changes:

- Some runes decay faster
    
- Old stabilizers resonate oddly
    
- Interstice hums are felt _through stone_
    

Gohlstout does not fear magic returning.  
They fear **it returning unevenly**.

---

## 4) Economy & Trade

### Exports

- Worked metals (steel, mithral alloys)
    
- Precision tools
    
- Structural stone
    
- Reinforced mechanisms
    
- Naval fittings
    

### Imports

- Grain
    
- Timber
    
- Luxury goods
    
- Rare reagents (sparingly)
    

Trade philosophy:

> “We sell reliability.”

Most nations rely on Gohlstout more than they admit — especially Mehrendt.

---

## 5) Military & Security

### The Stoneward Hosts

Heavy infantry trained for:

- Tunnel warfare
    
- Siege defense
    
- Controlled retreats
    

They rarely march far — but they never lose ground they hold.

### The Vaultguard

Elite defenders of sealed zones and Runeholds.

- Authorized to kill without explanation
    
- Often act before threats are visible
    

### Naval Presence (Western Peninsula)

Gohlstout maintains a **small but formidable fleet** in its western bay.

- Armored hulls
    
- Short-range, high-durability ships
    
- Used for trade protection, not conquest
    

---

## 6) Foreign Relations

### Mehrendt

- Transactional, tense, necessary
    
- Gohlstout distrusts Mehrendt’s Veinwatch
    
- Maintains contracts rather than alliances
    

### Fey’thra

- Mutual respect
    
- Shared water governance via Mirrorwake Lake
    
- Quiet information exchange on Interstice fluctuations
    

### Avalon

- Viewed as unstable but sincere
    
- Occasional covert support
    
- Dwarves understand _desperation_ better than most
    

---

## 7) Internal Fault Lines

1. **Sealed Knowledge**  
    Younger dwarves want access to Runeholds  
    Elders remember _why they were sealed_
    
2. **Surface Drift**  
    Some clans increasingly favor surface living and trade  
    Others see this as cultural erosion
    
3. **The Deep Frozen Signals**  
    Gohl-Fhronim’s glaciers are shifting faster than models predict  
    Runeholders fear something is _moving beneath the ice_
    
4. **Infrastructure Strain**  
    Old stabilizers hum more often  
    Repairs last shorter intervals  
    Stone “remembers stress” differently now
    

---

## 8) Key Locations (Gohlstout Anchors)

### 🏔️ Gohl-Khazir

Political, industrial, and cultural heart.

### ❄️ Gohl-Fhronim (The Deep Frozen)

Northern glacial mountains.

- Seasonal melt feeds Mirrorwake Lake
    
- Rune pylons embedded in ice
    
- Reports of cavern collapses beneath glaciers
    

### ⚙️ The Underflow Nexus

Where subterranean waters converge before reaching the Valecurrent.

- Heavily guarded
    
- Critical to all downstream nations
    

### 🛠️ Ironwake Bay

Western bay and naval hub.

- Shipyards carved into cliffs
    
- Heavy trade traffic
    
- Most “worldly” dwarven settlement
    

### 🚪 The Sealed Helms

Collapsed tunnel network sealed during the Fracture.

- Still warm
    
- Still humming
    
- Absolutely forbidden
    

---

## 9) Adventure Pressure in Gohlstout

Gohlstout draws players into:

- Ethical engineering dilemmas
    
- Secrets kept for _good reasons_
    
- Conflicts between safety and progress
    
- The realization that stability has a cost
    

If Mehrendt wants to **control** the future  
and Fey’thra wants to **listen** to it,  
Gohlstout wants to **survive it**.

---

# DM-Only Insight: Gohlstout’s Role in the Endgame

Gohlstout is the nation most likely to:

- Enable **Preservationist antagonists**
    
- Resist uncontrolled Ascension
    
- Become the battleground for final stabilization attempts
    

If a final antagonist wants to:

- Seal the Interstice forever
    
- Build a world that cannot change again
    
- Make the Fracture the _last_ catastrophe
    

They will need Gohlstout.

And Gohlstout will argue — bitterly — about whether that’s a mercy or a prison.
