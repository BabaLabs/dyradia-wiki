# Gohl-Khazir — _The Deep Hold_

**Type:** Capital Hold / Subterranean City  
**Nation:** Gohlstout  
**Region:** Eastern mountains (temperate stonefolds)  
**Known For:** Ledger Courts, deep archives, mastercraft, civic engineering  
**Strategic Value:** Economic engine + historical authority + strategic sanctuary  
**Triad Weather:** Stasis and Convergence both find allies here (order and continuity)

### Overview

Gohl-Khazir is a capital you feel more than you see: a city carved into enduring stone, designed to outlast eras rather than impress them. Its halls are organized with deliberate intent—districts aligned to function, circulation, and security. To outsiders it can feel severe. To dwarves it feels like safety made architectural.

### Quick Facts

- Called **Gohl-Khazir** in Dwarven, **The Deep Hold** in Common
    
- Home to the **Ledger Courts** and the **Vaulted Record**
    
- Famous for mastercraft, civic engineering, and public works
    
- Outsider trade is welcomed, but access is structured and monitored
    
- A culture of contracts, precedent, and recorded obligation shapes daily life
    

### City Character (Public)

- **The Ledger Steps:** courts, registries, arbitration halls, civic auditors
    
- **Vaulted Record:** public archives + restricted collections under formal petition
    
- **Forge-Rings:** smithing districts, artificer halls, guild enclaves
    
- **Deep Markets:** secured trade floors, bonded warehousing, caravan contracts
    
- **Stoneward:** defenses, gateholds, inspection corridors
    

### What People Say (In-World)

> “In Gohl-Khazir, the walls remember.”  
> “A dwarf’s word is stone. The Deep Hold just keeps the quarry.”
