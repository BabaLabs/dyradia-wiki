Four nations, and the contested ground between them.

**[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Mehrendt|Mehrendt]]** — the bureaucratic centre. Law, ministries, and paperwork used as a weapon. Its capital moved after the Fracture and it has never entirely stopped flinching.

**[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Gohlstout|Gohlstout]]** — mountains, engineering, and long institutional memory. Records everything, commits to nothing.

**[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Fey'thra|Fey'thra]]** — layered forest civilisation. Canopy cities, open commonwealths, and a very old relationship with things that don't answer to people.

**[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Avalon|Avalon]]** — no fixed cities. Nomadic tribes and wandering militias moving on cyclic routes across barren crags. Believes magic should not be controlled, and has paid for believing it.

**[[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Cross-Border/index|Cross-Border]]** — the seas, the free cities, and the places nobody can quite claim. Including [[Lore/Current Lore/Current Places & Events/Cross-Border/Dawnmere|Dawnmere]], which is where everything eventually passes through.
