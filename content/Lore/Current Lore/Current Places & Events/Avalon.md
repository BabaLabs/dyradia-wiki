# AVALON (Modern Era)

### “A People Who Refused to Disappear”

**Capital:** None (by design)  
**Landscape:** Arid foothills, badlands, high desert valleys  
**Dominant Feature:** **The Fractured Spires**  
**Common Description:** _“Unsettled.”_  
**Truth:** _Uncontainable._

Avalon is not a country in the way others understand the word.  
It is a **confederation of intent**, not territory.

---

## 1) Government

**Form:** Decentralized Coalition of Free Holds  
**Ruling Body:** None permanent  
**Decision Method:** War Councils convened _only when necessary_

Avalon learned a brutal lesson during and after the Fracture:

> **Centralized power is easy to corrupt, easy to destroy, and easy to co-opt.**

So they removed it.

Each Avalonian settlement (called a **Hold**) governs itself, but all share:

- Mutual defense pacts
    
- Open migration
    
- A shared oath of autonomy
    

When unity is required, leaders gather in a **War Council** — temporary, binding, and disbanded once the crisis ends.

No throne.  
No capital.  
No single head to cut off.

---

## 2) Power Centers (Such As They Are)

### ⚔️ The War Councils

- Assembled for invasion, existential threat, or major Interstice events
    
- Decisions are unanimous or not made at all
    
- Authority expires when the council dissolves
    

The most dangerous Avalonian leaders are the ones who **refuse to stay in power**.

---

### 🩸 The Bloodbound Compacts

Informal but sacred oaths between Holds.

- Sworn in blood, ash, or stone
    
- Breaking one is the fastest way to become an exile
    

These compacts replace laws.

---

### 🕊️ The Quiet Hands

Not an organization — a _role_.

The Quiet Hands:

- Negotiate with foreign powers
    
- Smuggle refugees
    
- Sabotage threats before they become wars
    

No insignia.  
No records.  
No recognition.

If someone knows a Quiet Hand by name, that Quiet Hand is already dead.

---

## 3) Magic Climate

**Public stance:** “Magic is survival.”  
**Private truth:** “Magic owes us nothing.”

Avalonian magic is:

- Practical
    
- Dirty
    
- Improvised
    
- Often learned the hard way
    

They:

- Don’t regulate magic
    
- Don’t romanticize it
    
- Don’t fear it
    

Post-Fracture realities:

- Magic manifests unevenly
    
- Some Holds ban certain practices
    
- Others lean hard into them
    

Avalon produces more _wild talent_ than anywhere else — and loses more to burnout, exile, or worse.

---

## 4) Economy & Trade

Avalon does not produce wealth.  
It produces **endurance**.

### Exports

- Mercenaries
    
- Scouts
    
- Survivalists
    
- Information
    
- Black-market goods
    

### Imports

- Grain
    
- Tools
    
- Medicine
    
- Ammunition
    
- Salt
    

Trade is:

- Informal
    
- Dangerous
    
- Often one-sided
    

Mehrendt profits from Avalon’s labor.  
Avalon knows it — and keeps score.

---

## 5) Military & Security

### 🛡️ Free Companies

Avalonian fighters operate in **bands**, not armies.

- Highly mobile
    
- Terrain-adapted
    
- Excellent at asymmetrical warfare
    

They do not hold territory.  
They deny it to others.

---

### 🏹 The Ashrunners

Elite scouts and skirmishers.

- Navigate the Fractured Spires
    
- Move through Interstice-scarred land
    
- Report on things that _should not exist_
    

Ashrunners have the highest casualty rate in Dyradia — and the highest cultural prestige.

---

## 6) Foreign Relations

### Mehrendt

- Viewed as manipulative but predictable
    
- Avalon despises the Veinwatch
    
- Border conflicts around the **Valecurrent** are constant
    

Avalon claims the river as ancestral.  
Mehrendt claims it by coin and contract.  
Neither is backing down.

---

### Fey’thra

- Complicated kinship
    
- Fey’thra shelters Avalonian refugees
    
- Avalon accuses Fey’thra of _waiting instead of acting_
    

Still — if Avalon is starving, Fey’thra feeds them.

---

### Gohlstout

- Mutual respect
    
- Quiet cooperation
    
- Dwarves sell tools that don’t break
    

Gohlstout does not ask questions.  
Avalon does not forget that.

---

## 7) Internal Fault Lines

1. **Fragmentation**  
    Some Holds want greater unity.  
    Others see that as the first step toward tyranny.
    
2. **The Next Generation**  
    Young Avalonians did not live through the Fracture.  
    They question old grudges — and old caution.
    
3. **The Church’s Ghost**  
    The Church of Prim did real damage in Avalon.  
    Some still hunt its remnants obsessively.  
    Others want to let the past die.
    
4. **Beyond the Interstice**  
    Expeditions vanish.  
    Survivors return… changed.  
    Some Holds want answers.  
    Others want the frontier sealed forever.
    

---

## 8) Key Locations (Avalon Anchors)

### 🏜️ The Fractured Spires

A massive, broken mountain range.

- Stone sheared at impossible angles
    
- Interstice scars visible to the naked eye
    
- No consistent maps
    

The Spires are both shield and curse.

---

### 🏕️ Ashenfall

A major gathering Hold.

- Temporary by design
    
- Moves every few years
    
- Traditional site for War Councils
    

---

### 🌊 Valecurrent Crossing

A fortified river passage constantly contested with Mehrendt.

- Trade
    
- Smuggling
    
- Sabotage
    
- Bloodshed
    

If war starts, it starts here.

---

### 🕯️ The Ember Shrines

Scattered memorial sites.

- Not religious
    
- Names of the dead carved into stone
    
- New names added every year
    

---

### 🌫️ The Far Reaches (Beyond the Interstice)

The edge of known land.

- Sky turns dark and earthen
    
- Sound carries strangely
    
- Time is unreliable
    

Avalon has gone farther than anyone else.  
And paid for it.

---

## 9) Adventure Pressure in Avalon

Avalon pulls players into:

- Moral clarity without comfort
    
- Freedom with consequences
    
- Decisions where neutrality is impossible
    
- Conflicts where _someone_ must lose
    

If Mehrendt wants to **control**,  
Fey’thra wants to **listen**,  
and Gohlstout wants to **endure**…

Avalon wants to **choose**.

Even if it hurts.

---

# DM-Only Insight: Avalon’s Role in the Endgame

Avalon is the nation most likely to:

- Create the **Harbinger of Renewal**
    
- Produce tragic heroes
    
- Break alliances to prevent worse outcomes
    
- Force the party to pick a side they don’t like
    

If the final antagonist becomes:

- A revolutionary
    
- An ascendant figure
    
- A necessary evil
    

Avalon is where they will be born — or broken.
