# Zephyr Feeding Protocol

A reusable handling approach for when Zephyr needs to feed during a session. Designed to be:

- **player-consent-forward**
- **non-graphic**
- **plot-relevant**
- and give Zephyr a meaningful character moment without derailing the group

---

## The Tone to Aim For

Not "vampire scene." More like: **a veteran dealing with a necessary maintenance problem** under pressure.

---

## Best Insertion Point

When the party has paused after action. Zephyr's strain can show. A trusted NPC ([[Thorne]], a sympathetic infirmary lead, a sanctioned contact) can notice and handle it quietly — which builds trust and seeds the NPC's "protector" vibe.

---

## How to Present It (DM Script)

The NPC pulls Zephyr aside with minimal fuss:

> "You're running on fumes."
> "Whatever you need, you handle it here — privately, cleanly, and with consent. I won't have you make a desperate choice at the wrong time."

They offer **two options** (both valid):

### Option A: Infirmary "donation" (cleanest)

Border-institutions like [[Greenward Annex]] keep **medical blood stores** (for emergencies, transfusions, rituals, etc.). Some staff/students also volunteer for controlled donation (especially in border institutions used to strange cases).

- **No graphic detail.** You fade to black.
- **Roleplay hook:** Zephyr has to decide whether to accept help, and on what terms.

**After:** Zephyr is steady again. Remove any hunger-related disadvantage that was in play.

### Option B: Animal blood / sanctioned substitute (less intimate)

Workshop master or infirmary provides a **prepared substitute** (animal blood preserved for medical / arcane use). Not as satisfying, but it works.

- Adds a nice character beat: *"I don't want to involve people."*

---

## If Zephyr Refuses to Feed

That's allowed — but give it a consequence that's not punitive:

- He becomes **edgy / foggy** in the next scene.
- Mechanically: *one* check at disadvantage of his choice **or** he takes −1 on social rolls until he feeds.
- It reinforces stakes without forcing behavior.

---

## Consent + Table Safety (DM-Facing One-Liner)

When it comes up, you can say OOC in one sentence:

> "We'll handle this fade-to-black and consensually — no graphic detail."

That keeps it comfortable for everyone.

---

## Optional Tie-In: A "Direction" Beat

If Zephyr feeds at a sanctioned location (either method), drop a subtle sensation:

> For a heartbeat, the world feels *quietly aligned* — like the wards are tuned to prevent desperate mistakes.

That seeds the broader theme: **places and systems shaping people** (Stasis / Convergence / Renewal) without explaining anything.
