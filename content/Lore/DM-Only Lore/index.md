> [!danger] Stop and read this before going further
> Everything below this page is **DM-facing material**. Antagonists and what they
> actually want. Plans in motion. The machinery underneath things the party has
> only seen the edges of. In several places, the answer to a question someone at
> the table is actively trying to work out.
>
> **If you're playing in the campaign**, this is the section that can genuinely
> cost you something. Not lore trivia — the reveals themselves.
>
> **If you're not playing**, or you're here for a one-off, or you just want to
> know how the world actually works: this is the best material on the site, and
> you should read all of it.

I'm not locking it. You're adults and I trust you. But I can't un-spoil a thing
once it's seen, so decide on purpose rather than by drifting.

Every page in here — and every DM-facing page elsewhere on the site — carries the
same red banner at the top. If you see it, that's your cue.
