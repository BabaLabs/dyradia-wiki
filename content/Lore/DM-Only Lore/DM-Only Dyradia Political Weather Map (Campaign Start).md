## A. Mehrendt — The Peninsula Kingdom

### A1) Dawnmere (Tri-Nation Trade Hub) — “Stability’s Showcase”

**Dominant Pressure:** **Stasis (Seraphel)**  
**Secondary Pressure:** Convergence (scholars reacting to constraints)

**What’s happening right now**

- The Crown Courts are rolling out a **Unified Stability Code** (pitched as modernizing post-Fracture law).
    
- “Magic licensing” is framed like medicine: _certification saves lives._
    
- A handful of **quiet disappearances** among fringe academics and unregistered hedge-mages… with no public panic, because the system assures everyone it’s handled.
    

**Who’s active here**

- **Magistrate Elowen Rhyss** (legal architecture, extradition, compliance)
    
- Seraphel’s administrative allies and sympathetic reform councils
    

**Encounter shapes (non-railroad)**

- A harmless-seeming healer arrested for “unstable practice”
    
- A trial where the party is _right_, but the law is _airtight_
    
- A patron offers the party “official authorization” (a leash disguised as access)
    

**If ignored, 3–6 session drift**

- Code passes → enforcement normalized → citizens begin reporting neighbors “for safety”
    

---

### A2) River/Estuary Trade Choke (Mehrendt–Avalon Border)

**Dominant Pressure:** **Stasis**  
**Secondary Pressure:** Renewal (resentment → radicalization)

**What’s happening right now**

- Mehrendt claims the river for trade; Avalon sees it as colonial chokehold.
    
- Checkpoints aren’t brutal — they’re _procedural_. That’s what makes them effective.
    
- Smugglers thrive; so do “stability contractors.”
    

**Who’s active**

- **Warden-Colonel Dareth Kain** (task forces / stabilization raids)
    
- Merchant guilds funding “security”
    

**Encounter shapes**

- A “routine inspection” that escalates when a PC pings as “anomalous”
    
- A border town that depends on bribery to survive
    
- A missing caravan where the truth is politically inconvenient
    

**Drift**

- Checkpoints harden → accidental riot → perfect pretext for more “temporary” authority
    

---

### A3) Church-Island (Old Vatican Analog)

**Dominant Pressure:** **Stasis**  
**Secondary:** Renewal (secretly, in the desperate)

**What’s happening right now**

- The island is still devout and influential, but diminished, scarred, and proud.
    
- Aid missions operate outward: food, shelter, “moral guidance.”
    
- Certain relic-vaults remain sealed… and rumor doesn’t exist here — only **silence**.
    

**Who’s active**

- Church hardliners and “humanitarian arms” aligned with Stasis values
    
- (Later) a path for Seraphel’s doctrine to gain legitimacy without being “the Church”
    

**Encounter shapes**

- A genuinely kind priest who still believes suppression is mercy
    
- A relic theft blamed on “heretics,” but the truth points elsewhere
    
- Refugees who say the island is safe… unless you ask the wrong questions
    

**Drift**

- Island diplomacy grows louder → soft power returns → “moral panic” becomes political capital

## B. Fey’thra — The Verdant Confluence of Hidden Cities

### B1) Syl’Feyra (Capital) & the Deep Canopy

**Dominant Pressure:** **Renewal (Elyndra)**  
**Secondary:** Convergence (scholars flirting with forbidden synthesis)

**What’s happening right now**

- Groves report **stronger-than-expected seasonal magic**.
    
- Council factions argue: contain it vs nurture it.
    
- Outsiders are watched politely; Fey’thrans don’t forbid, they _redirect_.
    

**Who’s active**

- Elyndra’s sympathizers in fringe circles
    
- **Caelith Rootbound** moving through settlements (not the capital center)
    

**Encounter shapes**

- A grove offering “healing rites” with unspoken costs
    
- Fey’thran wardens asking the party to handle an anomaly quietly
    
- A court invitation that’s actually a test of restraint
    

**Drift**

- A few “miracle zones” become politically protected → isolationism rises
    

---

### B2) Marshwoods & Swamplands (Hidden roads, forgotten shrines)

**Dominant:** **Renewal**  
**Secondary:** None (until it becomes a crisis)

**What’s happening**

- Crops survive in impossible soil.
    
- Fevers end overnight.
    
- But people’s dreams change. Children start drawing symbols they’ve never learned.
    

**Who’s active**

- **Caelith Rootbound** (evangelist/ritual architect)
    
- Smaller Renewal “saints” who don’t know they’re part of a movement
    

**Encounter shapes**

- A village that _adores_ their mystic and refuses outside aid
    
- A rite that saves one person but subtly alters another
    
- A “missing child” that isn’t missing — they _walked into the marsh_ willingly
    

**Drift**

- The region becomes self-defending → outsiders treated as threats to “the Gift”
    

---

### B3) The Great Lake (Shared Neutral Water) — THE LAKE PORT ANCHOR

**Dominant:** **Convergence emergent**  
**Secondary:** Renewal / Stasis both competing for influence

**What’s happening right now**

- This is neutral ground: trade, fishing, pilgrim traffic, quiet diplomacy.
    
- People are exhausted by ideology, so they reward “solutions.”
    
- Conflicts don’t escalate here. They dissolve. It feels like relief.
    

**Who’s active**

- **The Anchorbearer** (quiet fixer, mediator, stabilizer)
    
- **The Resonant Chorus** beginnings: a few dock workers, clerks, and innkeepers who “just want peace”
    

**Encounter shapes**

- A feud ends instantly after a “helpful stranger” speaks with both sides
    
- A tavern where everyone repeats the same phrase unconsciously
    
- A missing argument: the party can’t figure out why people stopped caring
    

**Drift**

- The port becomes famously safe → decision-making centralizes around one figure → subtle loss of plurality begins

## C. Gohlstout — The Deep Hold & the Frozen Range

### C1) Gohl-Khazir (Capital, eastern holds)

**Dominant:** **Stasis-adjacent** (preservationists)  
**Secondary:** Convergence (engineers, geometers, Interstice topology)

**What’s happening**

- Dwarves treat instability like engineering: measure, reinforce, contain.
    
- They are wary of both Elyndra’s chaos and Mehrendt’s bureaucratic overreach.
    
- Their concern isn’t politics — it’s whether the mountains stay standing.
    

**Who’s active**

- Conservative hold councils (stability through tradition)
    
- Quiet Convergence-minded “geometers” who want a unified model of reality
    

**Encounter shapes**

- A mining “accident” that looks like a planar shear
    
- A dwarven council hiring outsiders to avoid political fallout
    
- A sealed shaft that hums with wrongness
    

**Drift**

- Gohlstout begins building “permanent anchors” → aligns with Seraphel’s doctrine by practicality, not ideology
    

---

### C2) Gohl-Fhronim (“Deep Frozen”) & melt-fed floodplains

**Dominant:** **Neutral, pragmatic**  
**Secondary:** Renewal pressure (water, seasonal flooding, life)

**What’s happening**

- Meltwater patterns are shifting (subtle omen).
    
- Fey’thra and Gohlstout rely on the same flows, creating cooperation and tension.
    
- Rumors of “singing ice” or “warm veins” (Interstice bleed) begin.
    

**Encounter shapes**

- A seasonal flood uncovers ancient stonework
    
- A caravan route becomes unsafe for non-obvious reasons
    
- Dwarves ask the party to investigate without “making it a story”
    

**Drift**

- Cooperative treaties intensify → Convergence accelerates via shared infrastructure

## D. Avalon — The Arid Foothills & Fractured Spires

### D1) Border Settlements (river-adjacent)

**Dominant:** **Renewal reaction** (resentment → radicalization)  
**Secondary:** Stasis enforcement pressure

**What’s happening**

- People feel squeezed by Mehrendt’s chokehold.
    
- They don’t trust anyone with “law,” but they _do_ trust results.
    
- Small militant cells form—not always Elyndra-aligned, but compatible.
    

**Who’s active**

- **Ashcaller Veyra** can operate near here when she wants spectacle
    
- Local warbands with varying ideology
    

**Encounter shapes**

- A revolt that starts as trade dispute and becomes ideological
    
- A “hero” leader asking the party to choose sides
    
- A stability patrol that’s technically lawful and morally awful
    

**Drift**

- One violent incident becomes justification for wider suppression → perfect for Seraphel’s expansion narrative
    

---

### D2) The Fractured Spires (inhospitable range)

**Dominant:** **Mythic pressure** (late-game potential)  
**Secondary:** Renewal (if you want it)

**What’s happening**

- People vanish in the Spires. Not rumored. Just… gone.
    
- Old battlefield scars still exist in the stone.
    
- There are routes only the desperate take.
    

**Encounter shapes**

- “Nothing” for a long time… until it’s not nothing
    
- The kind of place where a single decision can matter for years
    

**Drift**

- Becomes a corridor to “Beyond the Interstice” if your campaign goes that far

## E. Beyond the Interstice — The Fractured Lands (Ungoverned)

**Dominant:** **All trajectories are possible** (this is your endgame canvas)

**What’s happening**

- The sky is wrong.
    
- Geography doesn’t obey ordinary mapping.
    
- This is where Convergence, Renewal, or Stasis can become undeniable.
    

**Encounter shapes**

- Only when you _want_ mythic escalation
    
- This is your “final act” stage, not your early game
