# Elowen Rhyss

## Player-Visible Tier (DC 10–25)

### Magistrate Elowen Rhyss — Crown Courts, Mehrendt

**DC 10:** A calm, brilliant reformer in the legal system; famous for “making chaotic times governable.”  
**DC 15:** Oversees extraditions and compliance rulings; her signature ends careers quietly.  
**DC 20:** People fear her politely. If she’s involved, outcomes feel predetermined.  
**DC 25:** She’s rumored to have authored the most aggressive definitions of “unstable magic” in circulation.

## Foothold Detail

### Lieutenant 1: **Magistrate Elowen Rhyss**

**Role:** Legal Architect & Extradition Authority  
**Public Face:** Calm, compassionate reformer  
**True Function:** Turns emergency powers into permanent law

**What She Does**

- Drafts post-crisis legislation
    
- Defines what counts as “unstable magic”
    
- Authorizes detentions _without trials_
    
- Creates inter-nation compliance agreements
    

She is the reason people vanish _legally_.

**First Player Contact**

- A quest involving a mage arrested “for their own safety”
    
- A trial where the law is airtight — and horrifying
    
- Rhyss is polite, articulate, and absolutely convinced she’s saving lives
    

**Narrative Echo**  
She is the Church of Prim’s **dogma**, translated into paperwork.
