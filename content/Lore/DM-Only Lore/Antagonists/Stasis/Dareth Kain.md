# Dareth Kain

## Player-Visible Tier (DC 10–25)

### Warden-Colonel Dareth Kain — Stabilization Task Forces

**DC 10:** A decorated veteran who responds to magical incidents and unrest.  
**DC 15:** Leads raids, seizures, and “containment.” He’s known for being efficient and “not cruel about it.”  
**DC 20:** His units make scenes go quiet fast—witnesses get scattered, reports get sealed.  
**DC 25:** He’s been seen near operations that later “officially never happened.”

## Foothold Detail

### Lieutenant 2: **Warden-Colonel Dareth Kain**

**Role:** Enforcement & Suppression  
**Affiliation:** Veinwatch-adjacent task forces  
**Public Face:** Decorated veteran, tired but honorable  
**True Function:** Makes suppression _normal_

**What He Does**

- Leads stabilization raids
    
- Oversees suppression engines
    
- Executes “necessary losses”
    
- Trains next-generation Veinwatch operatives
    

He hates what he does — which makes him effective.

**First Player Contact**

- He’s sent to “clean up” a situation the party is involved in
    
- He offers them a chance to walk away
    
- If they fight, he fights clean and efficiently
    

**Narrative Echo**  
He is the soldier who believes the Fracture happened because _someone hesitated_.
