# The Resonant Chorus

## Player-Visible Tier (DC 10–25)

### The Resonant Chorus (phenomenon)

**DC 10:** Some places are “too peaceful.”  
**DC 15:** Communities near certain stabilizers become unnervingly aligned—same phrases, same priorities.  
**DC 20:** Ambition fades. Dissent fades. Crime fades. So does _edge_.  
**DC 25:** Scholars have no shared terminology for it, which is unusual—like language itself slides off the subject.

---

## Territory Placement

**The Resonant Chorus**

- **Where:** radiating outward from Anchorbearer’s sphere (a few villages, then a small city ward)
    
- **Reach:** social/psychological alignment, not “mind control,” more like collective fatigue crystallizing

## Foothold Detail

### Lieutenant 2: **The Resonant Chorus**

**Role:** Collective, not individuals  
**What It Is**  
People who’ve spent too long near the Convergence:

- Think alike
    
- Decide alike
    
- Lose sharp individuality
    

They are peaceful.  
They are terrifying.

**First Player Contact**

- A town that’s harmonious… unnervingly so
    
- No crime
    
- No ambition
    
- No dissent
    

**Narrative Echo**  
This is the Fracture prevented by erasing friction.
