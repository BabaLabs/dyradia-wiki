## Foothold Detail

### Lieutenant 1: **The Anchorbearer**

**Role:** Stabilizer & Emissary  
**Public Face:** Soft-spoken problem solver  
**True Function:** Expands the Convergence’s influence

**What They Do**

- Resolve disputes effortlessly
    
- End conflicts without bloodshed
    
- Neutralize anomalies by proximity
    
- Encourage others to defer decisions
    

They never command.  
They _calm_.

**First Player Contact**

- A war that ends overnight
    
- Leaders agreeing instantly
    
- A sense that resistance feels… unnecessary
    

**Narrative Echo**  
They are what the world _wishes_ Bohrova had been.
