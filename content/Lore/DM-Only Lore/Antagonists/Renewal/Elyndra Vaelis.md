# Elyndra Vaelis

## Player-Visible Tier (DC 10–25)

### Elyndra Vaelis — “The Green Ascendant”

**DC 10 (Religion/Arcana):** A radical figure blamed (or praised) for magic surges and “miracle events.”  
**DC 15:** Seen as liberator by outcasts; seen as a terrorist by stability factions.  
**DC 20:** Their message spreads like wildfire after disasters—especially where law and aid fail.  
**DC 25:** Some claim Elyndra survived multiple Interstice anomalies. No one can prove it.

## DM Internal — Identity, Philosophy, Methods

## II. THE HARBINGER OF RENEWAL

### _“The world is healing. Stop trying to cage it.”_

**Theme:** Liberation → Transcendence → Catastrophe  
**End State:** A new world born violently

---

### Identity

**Public Name:** **Elyndra Vaelis**  
**Known As:**

- The Green Ascendant
    
- The Voice Beneath the Roots
    
- The One Who Remembers Tomorrow
    

**True Role:** The world’s fever dream given shape

Elyndra is charismatic, radiant, and terrifyingly sincere.

They believe the Fracture was not a tragedy—  
but a **failed transformation**.

---

### Origin

Elyndra was:

- Fey’thran-born or deeply Fey-aligned
    
- A scholar, seer, or ritualist
    
- Present at multiple Interstice anomalies and survived
    

They discovered something forbidden:

> The Interstice is not broken.  
> It is _restrained_.

And restraint is what’s killing the world slowly.

---

### Philosophy

Elyndra believes:

- The Dyra were never meant to be distant
    
- Awoken were prototypes, not aberrations
    
- The Fracture was the world trying to evolve
    

Their belief is intoxicating:

> “Suffering is not the cost of change.  
> Fear is.”

They want the world to _finish becoming_.

---

### Methods

- Reopening dormant Interstice channels
    
- Cultivating Awoken-like resonance
    
- Encouraging fey bleed and planar overlap
    
- Rallying the desperate, the oppressed, the forgotten
    

They don’t conquer.  
They **invite**.

---

### Early Signs

- Regions where magic grows stronger but unpredictable
    
- Fey behaving with open purpose
    
- Awoken myths resurfacing
    
- “Miraculous” events with devastating side effects
    

Elyndra makes people feel _seen_.

---

### Failure States

If opposed early:

- Elyndra retreats deeper into myth
    
- Their influence spreads indirectly
    
- Their ideas outlive them
    

They become the BBEG if:

- The party consistently rejects containment
    
- Institutions are dismantled without replacements
    
- Power is embraced as self-expression
    

---

### Final Form

Elyndra does not rule.

They become:

- A living conduit
    
- A convergence of Dyra resonance
    
- The heart of a reality storm
    

The final battle is:

- Chaotic
    
- Mythic
    
- Full of collateral consequence
    

Victory means:

- Stopping transcendence mid-birth
    
- Accepting that some healing must remain incomplete

## Territory & Active Pressure

## Elyndra Vaelis — Harbinger of Renewal

### Core Territory

- **Fey’thra (Marshwoods + hidden routes around Syl’Feyra):** recruitment, sanctuaries, ritual infrastructure.
    
- **The Great Lake + Southflow River:** Renewal spreads along water and communities that rely on it.
    
- **Avalon Fringe + Foothills:** where “liberation” rhetoric lands hardest.
    

### Active Pressure (Start-of-campaign)

- Small regions are experiencing **miracle boons with a cost**: crop recovery + strange births, healing + altered temperament, “blessings” + escalating instability.
    
- A “movement” isn’t visible in cities—what’s visible is **villages quietly choosing a different kind of survival**.
    
- One or two stabilization sites have been sabotaged in ways that look like “accidents”… but too clean.
    

### Lieutenants – Placement

**Caelith Rootbound**

- **Where:** Lake communities + Fey’thran fringe settlements (places that love him)
    
- **Reach:** soft power—he turns people into believers before anyone realizes there’s a doctrine
    

**Ashcaller Veyra**

- **Where:** places where Stasis infrastructure is being installed (or about to be)
    
- **Reach:** catalytic strikes—forces the world to react publicly
    

**The Verdant Threshold** (optional)

- **Where:** marsh-to-ruin transition zone near the edges of “Beyond the Interstice”
    
- **Reach:** a growing “beautiful wound” that rewrites reality slowly

## Foothold Detail

## II. ELYNDRA VAELIS — THE HARBINGER OF RENEWAL

_(Change, Liberation, and the Willingness to Pay the Price)_

### How Elyndra Gained a Foothold

Elyndra didn’t recruit elites.

They **found people who were already broken by the system**.

- Refugees from suppression zones
    
- Fey’thran outcasts
    
- Failed Awoken aspirants
    
- Borderland survivors
    

They didn’t promise safety.  
They promised **meaning**.
