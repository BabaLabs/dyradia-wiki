# Caelith Rootbound

## Player-Visible Tier (DC 10–25)

### Caelith Rootbound — Ritualist & Speaker

**DC 10:** A gentle mystic who brings hope to struggling communities.  
**DC 15:** Places touched by Caelith often improve—then become protective, insular, and “devout.”  
**DC 20:** Communities around him accept strange costs with a calm conviction.  
**DC 25:** Scholars argue his rites resemble “pre-Fracture resonance theories,” reappearing in new language.

## Foothold Detail

### Lieutenant 1: **Caelith Rootbound**

**Role:** Ritual Architect & Evangelist  
**Public Face:** Gentle fey-touched mystic  
**True Function:** Makes radical change feel holy

**What He Does**

- Leads convergence rituals
    
- Teaches that suffering has purpose
    
- Frames casualties as “necessary shedding”
    
- Keeps Elyndra’s hands clean
    

He believes completely.

**First Player Contact**

- A village that _loves_ him
    
- A ritual that visibly helps some people
    
- Survivors who say: “We knew the risk”
    

**Narrative Echo**  
He is Bohrova’s curiosity, stripped of restraint.
