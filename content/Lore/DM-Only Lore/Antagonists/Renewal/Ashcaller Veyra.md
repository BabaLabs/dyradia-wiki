# Ashcaller Veyra

## Player-Visible Tier (DC 10–25)

### Ashcaller Veyra — Revolutionary Vanguard

**DC 10:** A scarred revolutionary blamed for destructive events tied to magic and suppression sites.  
**DC 15:** She doesn’t hide; she wants witnesses.  
**DC 20:** She targets symbols of “containment” and dares authorities to respond publicly.  
**DC 25:** Some veterans whisper she can _provoke_ latent magic in others by proximity.
