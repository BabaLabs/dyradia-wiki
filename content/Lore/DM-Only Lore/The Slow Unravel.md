# The Slow Unravel

The campaign-progression model for representing diminishing magic that gradually becomes unstable. Derived from [[State of Magic in Dyradia]].

The throughline: magic appears stable but is failing in increasingly visible ways, as something or someone reconnects severed planar chains the world is no longer built to handle.

---

## Early Campaign (Subtle, Deniable)

Things to sprinkle in quietly:

- A cantrip flickers oddly once, then works fine
- A magical torch dims sooner than expected
- An Identify spell gives correct results… but feels incomplete
- A ritual circle needs "adjustments" no one remembers having to make
- Old inscriptions reference effects that no longer occur

Nothing dangerous yet. Just… wrong. NPC reactions matter more than mechanics here.

---

## Mid-Campaign (Patterns Emerge)

This is where denial cracks:

- Wild magic-like surges occur **without sorcerers present**
- Spells occasionally produce unintended side effects
- Magical wards fail in specific regions
- Ley-adjacent areas show environmental changes
- Clerics receive conflicting divine signals

Importantly, these events are localized. They can be blamed on:

- Old ruins
- Relics
- "Residual Awoken energy"
- Heretical cult activity

Everyone argues. No one agrees why.

---

## Late Campaign (The Lie Breaks)

Now the truth can't be ignored:

- Magic becomes volatile near key locations
- Ancient sites "wake up"
- New Awoken-like phenomena appear — but wrong
- People survive things they shouldn't
- Others are consumed by magic they thought they controlled

This is where the throughline becomes literal:

> "The severed chains are being reconnected — and the world cannot contain it."

---

## Pacing Principles

- Symptoms should feel like environmental weather, not plot announcements
- Multiple factions can offer competing explanations (this maps cleanly to the Triad antagonists — [[Seraphel Vaune]], [[Elyndra Vaelis]], [[The Living Convergence]])
- Escalation per session should be small but cumulatively visible
- Late-campaign volatility should make the antagonists' positions feel more or less reasonable to the party depending on which one they're leaning toward
