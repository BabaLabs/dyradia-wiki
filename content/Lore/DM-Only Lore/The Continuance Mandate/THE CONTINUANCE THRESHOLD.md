# THE CONTINUANCE THRESHOLD

### (Working title — rename later if you want)

This section defines **when the world decides a problem is no longer allowed to resolve naturally**.

Not morally.  
Not dramatically.  
**Structurally.**

---

## CORE AXIOM (Lock This In)

> **The Mandate does not respond to danger.  
> It responds to destabilization.**

People die all the time.  
Wars happen.  
Magic surges.  
Gods meddle.

None of that matters.

What matters is **continuity failure**.

---

## THE FOUR INVISIBLE THRESHOLDS

Think of these as **pressure gauges**, not switches.

The party can cross one and never know.  
They can hover near several.  
Only when enough pressure accumulates does _procedure_ begin.

---

## 🜂 THRESHOLD I — ARCHIVAL DISTORTION

_(Low-level, passive monitoring)_

**Definition:**  
The party uncovers, creates, or alters information that **should not exist together**.

Examples:

- Two mutually exclusive historical accounts both proven true
    
- Records that contradict sealed Church-era outcomes
    
- Proof that an Awoken existed post-Fracture
    
- Interstice artifacts that answer questions the world has agreed not to ask
    

🟢 **What happens:**  
Nothing visible.

Internally:

- A ledger is opened
    
- A file begins
    
- No names are written yet
    

This threshold is crossed **often** — even scholars do it accidentally.

This is _not danger_.

---

## 🜃 THRESHOLD II — NARRATIVE CONVERGENCE

_(Patterns emerge)_

**Definition:**  
Multiple destabilizing elements begin pointing toward **the same actors**.

Examples:

- The party repeatedly uncovers “lost” truths
    
- Different factions independently react to the same group
    
- Their actions start _resolving_ contradictions instead of creating them
    
- They become a through-line in suppressed histories
    

🟡 **What happens:**  
Lanterns activate — quietly.

- Trade routes shift around them
    
- Contacts go silent
    
- Access becomes inconsistent
    
- Investigations into their deeds stall
    

No threats.  
No pursuit.  
Just friction.

This is the **first moment the party might feel something is off** — but never identify _what_.

---

## 🜁 THRESHOLD III — CONTINUITY RISK

_(Authorization stage)_

**Definition:**  
The party is on track to **force a truth into the world** that cannot be absorbed gradually.

This includes:

- Public revelation of Bohrova-level truths
    
- Mass exposure of Interstice instability
    
- Political realignment that echoes pre-Fracture fault lines
    
- Destabilizing ascensions or proto-Destinies
    

This is the **danger zone**.

🟠 **What happens:**  
Arbiters convene.

Not to judge morality.  
To assess **containment options**.

At this stage:

- Names may be written — but not concluded
    
- Outcomes other than death are preferred
    
- Time is the primary variable
    

The party is still **not targeted**.

They are **evaluated**.

---

## 🜄 THRESHOLD IV — IRREVERSIBLE DISCLOSURE

_(Procedure begins)_

**Definition:**  
The party is about to make something **true in a way the world cannot undo**.

Not “big.”  
Not “evil.”  
**Permanent.**

Examples:

- A public act that reshapes belief systems
    
- An Awoken emergence that cannot be denied
    
- A mass ritual restoring a broken Interstice link
    
- Evidence that reframes the Fracture itself
    

🔴 **What happens:**  
Procedure begins.

Not “assassins.”  
Not “hunters.”

**Conclusion protocols.**

At this point:

- One or more individuals may be named
    
- Or assets, relationships, credibility, or access may be eliminated instead
    
- Death is _only one_ of many tools
    

This is the **only threshold** where the faction can ever intersect the party directly.

---

## WHY THIS IS FAIR (And Not Railroading)

- Players can cross thresholds unknowingly ✔
    
- Players can retreat, redirect, or delay ✔
    
- Players can _win_ without ever triggering Threshold IV ✔
    
- Players can change the world **slowly** and be allowed ✔
    

The Mandate punishes **speed and certainty**, not curiosity.

That’s the balance.

---

## WHAT ABSOLUTELY DOES _NOT_ TRIGGER THEM

Lock these in so you never feel tempted:

❌ Killing powerful enemies  
❌ Defeating major villains  
❌ Overthrowing corrupt rulers  
❌ Discovering ancient ruins  
❌ Becoming famous  
❌ Being strong  
❌ Being right

Heroes are allowed.

**Shortcuts are not.**

---

## THE MOST IMPORTANT DESIGN CHOICE YOU JUST MADE

This faction:

- Is not a consequence of being interesting
    
- Is not a punishment for success
    
- Is not a looming threat
    

It is a **failsafe** for when the story threatens to tear itself open.

Which means:

> If they ever appear, the party will _know_ something has gone very, very wrong — structurally.

Not morally.  
Not personally.

Structurally.
