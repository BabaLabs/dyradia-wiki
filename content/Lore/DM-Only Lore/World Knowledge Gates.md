# World Knowledge Gates

A growing collection of **PC-specific knowledge rulings** — what a given character knows about a given topic at a given check result.

Each ruling provides:

- **The trigger** — which PC, which check, which DC band
- **DM-side reasoning** — what the character actually knows and doesn't know, and why
- **Player-facing text** — language Jenna can read directly to the player to deliver the ruling without breaking immersion or revealing the DC

Rulings here are not exhaustive lore dumps. They're calibrated answers tuned to the *character*, the *check result*, and the *world's level of available knowledge*.

## Felix — History 7 — Do Dhampir Exist?

**PC:** Felix
**Check:** History
**Result:** 7
**Topic:** Whether dhampir exist as a category of being (separate from whether Zephyr is one)

### DM-Side Reasoning

For a 7 History, treat Felix as having heard vampire-adjacent campfire nonsense, but not actually knowing what a dhampir is in any reliable way.

Felix has heard stories about vampires.

Not scholarly stories. Not accurate ones. More like fear-stories told in bad places by tired people who wanted the dark to feel explainable.

- Stories about pale things.
- Hungry things.
- People who came back wrong.
- People who drank blood.
- People who looked alive until they didn't.

But the word *"dhampir"*?

That does not land cleanly.

Maybe he has heard it once. Maybe not. If he has, it was probably used like a rumor, insult, or monster-hunter word — not something anyone explained properly.

Felix would **not** know:

- that dhampir are a distinct kind of being,
- what causes someone to become one,
- whether they are born that way or made,
- whether they are undead, alive, cursed, or something in between,
- what they need to survive,
- or how common/rare they are.

At most, Felix might think:

> *"There are stories about people with vampire blood or vampire hunger. I thought those were mostly stories."*

So if Felix is looking at Zephyr, he does **not** get to conclude:

> *"Zephyr is a dhampir."*

He gets something much vaguer:

> *"Zephyr seems… wrong in a way I do not have good words for. Pale, controlled, hungry maybe. But I do not know what that means."*

### Player-Facing Version *(read to Felix's player)*

> You've heard vampire stories before — mostly ugly rumors, prisoner talk, campfire warnings, and things people say when they're scared of the dark.
>
> The word *"dhampir"* does not mean much to you. It sounds like the kind of term a scholar, hunter, or priest might use, but you do not know it clearly.
