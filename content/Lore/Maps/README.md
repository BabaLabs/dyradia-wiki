# Maps

Canonical visual reference for the Dyradia setting. Any map added here should be treated as **primary source** — where the map contradicts a text description, the map wins unless Jenna rules otherwise.

---

## Current Maps

### `DyradiaMap.jpg` — Primary Region Map (CURRENT/Campaign Dyradia)

![[DyradiaMap.jpg]]

The canonical CURRENT-era map of Dyradia's four core nations and the southeast wilderness. This is **post-Fracture political geography** — the borders, settlements, and named regions reflect the world of the campaign, not the pre-Fracture era.

> [!warning] Not to scale
> Adjacency and direction are accurate; **distance is not**. Dyradia is Asia/Russia-scale — roughly 5,000–7,000 miles corner to corner, or 18–24 months on foot in good conditions. The Node network is why anyone ever got anywhere.

**Nations depicted:**

- **Mehrendt** — central/northern portion; capital at CrownSpire (aka Valemarch); pre-Fracture capital was [[Nohlle]], destroyed during the Fracture era
- **Gohlstout** — northeast portion, mountainous; ranges: Gohl-Fhronim (north), Gohl-Khazir (east)
- **Fey'thra** — south-central portion; heavy forest; central settlement Syl'Feyra
- **Avalon** — southwest portion; distinctly arid/ochre terrain

**Regions & waters:**

- **The Aurelian Expanse** — northern ocean/water body
- **Crownwater Bay** — separates Mehrendt from Gohlstout
- **Fractured Lands** — southeast wilderness zone, post-Fracture desolation
- **"Beyond the Interstice"** — labeled in the smoke/miasma at the far southeast corner

**Named settlements & sites visible on the map:**

*Mehrendt:* Northwatch Haven, Waymarch, CrownSpire (capital), Stagspire, Crownwater Penitentiary (aka The Dreadworks), Lake Luminmere, Luminmere Collegium, Saltveil Commune, Fort Glasswake, The Pale Seat (modern name of [[Sanctum Primaris]] — the Church island), Dawnmere (aka [[Sahlen]]), Greensward Annex

*Gohlstout:* labeled but individual settlements not distinguished at overview scale

*Fey'thra:* Syl'Feyra (central) plus several unnamed forest settlements

*Avalon:* labeled but individual settlements not distinguished at overview scale

**Off-map / unrepresented on this file:**

- Regions south and east of the depicted area (Jenna's note: "to the south and eastern borders there is more, but this is the primary setting of the entire story")

## Notes for Chronicle Voice and Prose Voice

When writing scenes with geographic specificity, this map should be checked first for:

- Adjacency (which nations border which)
- Approximate distances between named settlements
- Terrain type at any given position
- The direction/orientation of travel between two points

Where the map is silent (interior geography of Gohlstout, Avalon, Fey'thra), the ten Mehrendt lore files under `Current Places & Events/Mehrendt/` and the parallel folders for other nations should be consulted before invention.
