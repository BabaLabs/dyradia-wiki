Canonical visual reference for Dyradia.

**[[Lore/Maps/README|The Primary Region Map]]** — campaign-era political geography of the four core nations and the south-eastern wilderness.

Adjacency and direction on it are accurate. **Distance is not.** Dyradia is roughly Asia-scale — 5,000 to 7,000 miles corner to corner, or eighteen to twenty-four months on foot in good conditions. The Node network is the only reason anybody ever got anywhere.
